Cookies

This page describes which cookies the website paklajutirisanariga.lv uses and how you can manage them. The website does not use advertising cookies and does not build visitor profiles for advertising.

Prepared by Paklāju Tīrīšana Rīgā, updated 10 November 2026. Sources: 1

What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a website saves in your browser. It lets the website remember, for example, your choice about cookies. Some cookies disappear when you close the browser, others stay for a set time. The browser’s local storage works in a similar way, and the same rules apply to it.

Which cookies we use

At present the website paklajutirisanariga.lv does not use cookies: there are no analytics, advertising or social media tools, and the request form works without cookies. That is why no cookie notice is shown on the website.

If we add visit statistics in the future, they will run only with your consent, a cookie notice with the option to change your choice will appear on the website, and this page will be updated.

Storing information on the user’s device is allowed with the user’s consent; consent is not needed where it is necessary to provide a service the user has requested. Source: Law on Information Society Services, Section 7.¹ (likumi.lv). Checked 03.11.2026.

Photos added to the form

If you add photos to the form, the browser temporarily saves them in the session storage of this tab, so that they are not lost while you take photos with your phone camera. This data stays on your device until you send the form or close the tab, and is not used for any other purpose. After sending, the website deletes it. This storage is necessary for the form function you requested, so consent for it is not required.

How to manage cookies in your browser

In the privacy settings of all common browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) you can:

  • view the stored cookies;
  • delete all or individual cookies;
  • block cookies from specific or all websites;
  • set cookies to be deleted when you close the browser.

Personal data

How we process the data and photos given in the form is described on the privacy policy page.

Changes

We update this page if the technical solutions used on the website change. Version: 10.11.2026.
