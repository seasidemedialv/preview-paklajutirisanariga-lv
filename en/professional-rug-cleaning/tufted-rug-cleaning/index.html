A synthetic tufted rug can be cleaned at home, but a wool or viscose tufted rug is cleaned in a workshop and brought back. The price is from €6/m² at home, and with pick-up from €10/m² or more, depending on the fibre.

How a tufted rug is built

In a tufted rug, the yarn is pulled through a fabric backing and fixed with latex glue, and a second fabric covers the glue from underneath. A hand-knotted rug has no glued layer. So “hand-tufted” and “hand-knotted” are two different rugs, even if they look similar on top.

How to tell: turn a corner over. If there is a smooth fabric underneath and the fringe is sewn on, it is usually a tufted rug. If there is no glued fabric underneath and the fringe comes out of the backing, see Persian and oriental rug cleaning.

What a glued backing does not tolerate

  • Heavy moisture and heat can weaken the glue, and the layers can start to separate.
  • Over the years a latex backing can become brittle, crumble or smell.
  • Tufts can fall out of a tufted rug.
  • A new rug with a latex backing may smell of ammonia at first.
  • A latex backing can stick to some new floor finishes, change their colour or crumble.

So do not soak a tufted rug in the bath or wash it in a washing machine. If dust or crumbs are already falling from the underside, photograph it: this kind of backing does not recover after cleaning. About washing at home: can a rug go in the washing machine.

Before cleaning

Add to the request photos of the top and the underside, a photo of the label if there is one, and the size in metres. Write whether there are stains, smell or pets, and whether crumbs fall from the underside. If the cleaning will be at home, take chairs, small tables and plant pots off the rug first, and write about heavy furniture on the rug in the request. Also write which floor the room is on, whether there is a lift and where a car can stop.

For a fresh stain, only blot with a clean cloth without rubbing, and do not pour a lot of water on it: moisture that reaches the glue can weaken it.

What to expect from tufted rug cleaning

Usually comes out

  • dust and everyday dirt in the pile;
  • fresh stains that have not soaked down to the backing.

May stay

  • a crumbling or brittle latex backing;
  • tufts falling out;
  • smell from the backing, if it comes from old glue;
  • places where the layers have already separated.

Frequently asked questions

Why do tufts fall out of a tufted rug?

In a tufted rug the yarn is pulled into the backing and held by glue, not by knots, so tufts can fall out. Cleaning does not stop it.

Can a tufted rug be cleaned at home?

If it is synthetic, usually yes. Wool and viscose tufted rugs are cleaned in a workshop and brought back. If you do not know the fibre, add photos, and a specialist will advise.

Crumbs are falling from the underside of the rug. Can it still be cleaned?

Cleaning does not restore a crumbling latex backing, and moisture can make it worse. Add photos of the underside, and a specialist tells you whether cleaning makes sense.

A new rug smells of ammonia. Does it need cleaning?

A new rug with a latex backing may smell of ammonia at first. It does not always mean the rug is dirty. Ventilate the room, and if the smell does not go away or is different, write about it in the request.
