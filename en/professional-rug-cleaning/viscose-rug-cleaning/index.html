Bamboo rug cleaning: it is usually viscose

Rugs sold as “bamboo silk” are usually made of viscose. The name “bamboo” says little about cleaning: what matters is the fibre the pile is made of. Such rugs look like silk, but when wet they become much weaker.

If the rug is made of bamboo slats or braided from plant fibres, that is a different matter: see jute and sisal rug cleaning. If the purchase documents say “silk”, compare with silk rug cleaning.

What happens to viscose when it gets wet

  • Wet viscose becomes much weaker than dry viscose and can change shape.
  • The pile can turn brown or yellow from water. High humidity and hot water make it worse.
  • Water and pressure can crush the pile permanently.
  • The pile must not be rubbed or steamed.

So a viscose rug needs controlled wet cleaning and full drying, which a living room floor does not give. A dry pile is brushed only in the direction of the pile.

Sources: Ohio State University fact sheet HYG-5538 (viscose when wet); Cleanfax, “Rayon: the fiber that just won’t go away” (browning, flattening); Brentano Fabrics care sheet (do not rub, do not steam, brush with the pile). Checked 10.11.2026.

If liquid has been spilled on a viscose rug

Blot the liquid with a clean, absorbent cloth from the edges of the stain towards the centre, without rubbing. Do not pour water on it to “rinse”, because on viscose extra moisture can leave a brown edge. Do not use hot water, steam or a hair dryer, and do not brush the pile while it is damp.

Then photograph the spot and write in the request what was spilled and whether you have already tried to clean it. About the methods and why steam does not suit viscose: dry, wet or steam carpet cleaning.

What to add to the request

  • The size in metres, measured without the fringe.
  • A photo of the label or a purchase description with the name of the fibre.
  • Photos of the top in raking light and of the underside.
  • Stains, yellow or brown areas, smell.
  • Whether the rug has already been cleaned and how.

Flattened areas and differences in shade are easier to see if the rug is photographed from two angles.

Frequently asked questions

Why did my viscose rug turn yellow after washing?

A viscose pile can turn brown or yellow from water, especially if there was a lot of moisture or the water was hot. Such areas may stay after cleaning, so add photos and write how the rug was washed.

Is a bamboo silk rug the same as viscose?

Usually yes. “Bamboo silk” is a common trade name for a viscose rug, and in cleaning it has the same risks: weakness when wet, browning and a flattened pile.

Can a viscose rug be steam cleaned?

No. A viscose pile must not be steamed or rubbed, because water and pressure can crush it permanently.

Will a flattened pile stand up again after cleaning?

That cannot be promised. A flattened viscose pile may stay, because viscose gets weak when wet and pressure deforms it.
