Why wool needs different cleaning from synthetics

Wool fibres have scales. In hot water they lift, and if the wool is rubbed or moved at the same time, the fibres catch on each other. This is how felt forms: the material becomes denser and shrinks, and usually it cannot be reversed.

Conservators name three more risks. Heat strips wool of its natural oils and weakens the threads. High humidity can damage the fibre and make the dyes bleed. Light dries the fibre and fades the colours. This is why a wool rug needs controlled wet cleaning and full drying, which a living room floor does not give.

Sources: UW-Extension “The Science of Felting” on felting; Canadian Conservation Institute “Basic care: carpets and rugs” on heat, humidity and light. Checked 10.11.2026.

Washing a wool rug at home: what you can and cannot do

At home you can vacuum regularly, rotate the rug from time to time so that it wears evenly, and blot a fresh stain with a clean cloth without rubbing. A blood stain is cleaned with cold water only, because heat sets it.

  • Do not wash a wool rug in a washing machine or soak it in the bath.
  • Do not use hot water or steam.
  • Do not use shop-bought carpet shampoo: the Canadian Conservation Institute advises against it for important rugs.
  • Do not dry it on a radiator or in direct sunlight.
  • Before using any product, test the colourfastness on a hidden spot, because the dyes can bleed.

Moths in a wool rug

Clothes moth larvae feed on wool and other materials of animal origin. They avoid light, so the parts of a rug under furniture and rugs kept in a dark place suffer most. Washington State University advises vacuuming and cleaning these places regularly and notes that insecticides are not an effective fix: in the long term only finding and removing the source helps.

If there are live moth larvae in the rug, it is a pest control job. If the moths are gone but there are eaten patches, add a photo: cleaning does not restore eaten patches. Carpet beetles also eat wool; for them, see pest control at dezinsekcijariga.lv.

Source: WSU PestSense, Casemaking clothes moth; Canadian Conservation Institute. Checked 10.11.2026.

Frequently asked questions

Can a wool rug be cleaned at my home?

No, wool rugs are cleaned only with pick-up. The rug is cleaned in a workshop and brought back, because wool needs controlled wet cleaning and full drying.

My wool rug shrank after washing. Can it be reversed?

Usually not. Felted wool becomes denser, and this cannot be reversed. Cleaning can remove dirt, but it does not restore the size.

Does cleaning kill moths?

We do not promise that. If there are live moth larvae in the rug, it is a pest control job. Afterwards, keep the clean rug in a dry place, covered with cotton fabric, and vacuum regularly under the furniture.

How do I measure a wool rug with a fringe?

Measure the length and width in metres without the fringe. For a round rug, give the diameter: the price is calculated by the square around it, so 2 m across is 4 m².
