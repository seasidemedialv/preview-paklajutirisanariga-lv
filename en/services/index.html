Carpets and rugs we clean at home

At home we clean fitted carpets and large synthetic rugs. Rug cleaning at home is for loose rugs made of polypropylene, polyester, nylon or acrylic. Fitted carpet cleaning is for soft floor covering in a flat, a stairwell or an office: it cannot be lifted, so it is cleaned on site only.

Before cleaning at home, remove chairs, small tables and flower pots from the rug. Mention heavy furniture on the rug in the request.

Rugs that must be cleaned in a workshop

Rug cleaning with pick-up is for rugs that can be rolled up: the rug is cleaned in a workshop and brought back. For wool, cotton, silk, viscose, hide, jute, hand-made and Persian rugs it is the only way (for jute, usually), because these fibres need controlled cleaning and full drying, which a living room floor cannot provide.

What each fibre can and cannot take is on the page professional rug cleaning. It also has separate pages for wool, silk, viscose, Persian and other rugs.

Stains, businesses and curtains

Carpet stain removal is the place to start if the main problem is coffee, wine, urine, smell or pet hair. Commercial carpet cleaning is for offices, hotels, restaurants, schools and property managers. Curtain cleaning is a separate service priced per m² of fabric. If you need both carpets and curtains cleaned, write that in one request.

What we do not do

  • Rug repairs, edging and fringe restoration.
  • Rug storage.
  • Rental (exchange) entrance mats and car mats.
  • Sofas, mattresses and chairs.

Frequently asked questions

What should I choose if I do not know where to clean the rug?

In the “Where to clean?” field of the form, tick that you are not sure and add photos from the top and from the underside. A specialist will recommend the right option based on the fibre and the size.

Can I request several rugs at once?

Yes. List all the rugs in one request with their sizes and photos. If some are cleaned at home and some with pick-up, say so for each rug.

Do you also clean car mats?

No. We clean indoor rugs, fitted carpets and curtains.
