Urine smell in a rug - what to do

How to remove cat urine from a rug: blot it at once, do not use ammonia or hot water, and look at the underside of the rug too, because the area of the smell can be up to three times larger than the visible stain. The smell of fresh urine on the surface can usually be reduced. If the urine has reached the backing or the floor, the smell can come back even after careful cleaning.

What to do in the first minutes

  1. Blot Put a clean cloth or paper towel on it and press until it no longer soaks anything up. Do not rub, because rubbing pushes the liquid deeper and spreads the stain.
  2. Cover it and weigh it down Put clean, absorbent material on the spot and press it down with something heavy until it dries.
  3. Find the whole area Smell around the stain too and lift a corner of the rug. A damp or dark area on the backing shows how far the urine has gone.
  4. Test the product If you use a product, first try it on a hidden spot, because it can change the colour.
  5. Take photos Photos of the top side and the underside will be useful if you have the rug cleaned later.

Sources: Canadian Conservation Institute on blotting and weighting; the Feline Veterinary Medical Association (Feline VMA), House Soiling handout, on the area of the smell and testing a product. Checked 07.11.2026 and 10.11.2026.

Why urine smell in a rug comes back

A rug has several layers: the pile, the backing, and the underlay or the floor under it. Urine can reach all these layers, but home cleaning usually reaches only the surface. So the smell can come back, especially if the urine has reached the backing.

What not to do, and why

  • Products containing ammonia: Feline VMA warns that to a cat they smell of urine and can attract the cat back.
  • Hot water and a hair dryer: on wool, hot water together with rubbing causes felting, and the rug can shrink; this usually cannot be reversed.
  • Chlorine bleach and strong products: they can change the colour.
  • Soaking the rug in the bath or using a lot of water: moisture goes into the backing and the floor, and the area of the smell can become larger.
  • Carpet shampoos on a valuable rug: conservators write that after them dirt sticks to the fibres faster.

Home recipes with baking soda, vinegar or ammonia differ between sources, and none of the sources shows that they are safe for a particular rug. If you still try something, test it on a hidden spot. Veterinary organisations, such as the ASPCA, mention enzyme products for pet urine. Test those on a hidden spot first too.

How to remove dog urine from a rug

The steps are the same: blot, do not rub, no heat, no ammonia, test the product on a hidden spot. If there have been accidents in several places, photograph each spot while they are still visible.

If the cat urinates repeatedly

For repeated urination or marking, the MSD Veterinary Manual advises first ruling out health causes with a vet. International Cat Care writes that a heavily soiled material stays attractive to a cat for marking even after cleaning, and if possible it is thrown out. An underlay that urine has reached may also have to be replaced.

Sources: MSD Veterinary Manual, Behavior problems of cats; International Cat Care, Urine spraying in cats; ASPCA, Urine marking in cats. Checked 07.11.2026.

Frequently asked questions

Does vinegar remove cat urine smell from a rug?

Sources on vinegar on rugs differ, and it is not proven that it is safe for every fibre. If you try it, test it on a hidden spot first. On wool and hand-made rugs the dyes can bleed.

How large is the area of the smell around a urine stain?

Feline VMA writes that the area of the smell can be up to three times larger than the visible stain. So look at and smell the place around it and the underside of the rug too.

Why does urine smell come back after cleaning?

Home cleaning usually reaches only the surface, but the urine can also be in the backing, the underlay or the floor. The smell can come back, especially if the urine has reached the backing.

Can dog urine be cleaned the same way as cat urine?

Yes, the first aid steps are the same: blot, do not rub, no heat and no ammonia. If a dog urinates repeatedly, it is also better to discuss the cause with a vet.

Can a urine stain change the colour of a rug?

It can. The product used to clean the stain can change the colour too, so do not use chlorine bleach or strong products. On wool and hand-made rugs the dyes can bleed, so the colourfastness must be tested on a hidden spot before wetting the rug.
