Rug cleaning with baking soda and vinegar

Rug cleaning with baking soda and vinegar is a common home tip, but the recipes differ between sources, and no source proves that baking soda or vinegar is safe or harmful for a particular rug. The result is set by the rug’s fibre, dyes and backing. If you try anyway, first read the label and test the product on a hidden spot.

What can be said safely and what cannot

In university and conservator material on stains and rugs, only the basics agree: blot the stain, do not rub, use cold water, do not heat, and test any product on a hidden spot first. On baking soda, vinegar and ammonia the advice differs, and none of it answers the main question: how exactly your rug’s fibre and colour will react.

So there are no recipes with amounts here. Instead there is what you can check: which rugs are at risk from wet home cleaning, how to do the test and when it is better to stop.

Sources: Canadian Conservation Institute, University of Georgia Extension, NMSU G-402 (home remedy advice differs; the basics agree: blot, cold water, no heat, test). Checked 10.11.2026 and 07.11.2026.

Testing on a hidden spot

  1. Read the label If the rug has a care label, a cross over a symbol means “do not”. If there is no label, photograph the underside: from it a specialist can judge the fibre and the backing.
  2. Choose a spot Choose a spot that is not visible: under a sofa, in a corner by the wall, or on the underside of the rug by the edge.
  3. A small amount Put a little of the product on a white cloth, not directly on the rug, and press lightly.
  4. Check the cloth If the cloth picks up colour, the rug’s dye bleeds. Then do not use the product.
  5. Let it dry When the spot is completely dry, look for any change in colour, sheen or pile.

Sources: University of Georgia Extension (test on a hidden spot for colourfastness, read the label first), GINETEX (care symbols). Checked 07.11.2026.

Cleaning a rug with vinegar: what to know about smell

Vinegar is mentioned as a home remedy for urine smell. Veterinary sources mention enzyme cleaners for pet urine, not vinegar or baking soda. The Feline Veterinary Medical Association (Feline VMA) warns that products containing ammonia smell of urine to a cat, and writes that the area of the smell can be up to three times larger than the visible stain. International Cat Care writes that a heavily soiled material stays attractive for repeat marking.

If urine has reached the backing, the underlay or the floor, cleaning the top of the rug may not end the smell, and the smell can come back. What to do at once: urine smell in a rug. Service: rug cleaning for urine.

Sources: Feline VMA, House Soiling (area of the smell, ammonia, test on a hidden spot), ASPCA (enzyme products), International Cat Care (a heavily soiled material stays attractive for repeat marking). Checked 07.11.2026.

What to do instead of baking soda and vinegar

  • Blot the stain at once with a clean cloth, without rubbing.
  • Dab lightly with a cloth dampened in cold water, without soaking.
  • Put on clean, absorbent material and weight it down until it dries.
  • Do not heat the spot with a hair dryer or an iron.
  • If the stain stays, do not put products on one after another, and send a photo.

Frequently asked questions

Can baking soda fade a rug?

This cannot be said in advance, because it depends on the rug’s dyes. New Mexico State University warns that an unsuitable product can make the dye bleed. So first do the test on a hidden spot with a white cloth.

Does vinegar remove urine smell from a rug?

Veterinary sources mention enzyme products for pet urine, not vinegar. If urine has reached the backing or the floor, the smell can come back even after cleaning. The smell can be reduced, but it cannot be promised that it will disappear.

Can a wool rug be cleaned with baking soda?

For wool the main risk is moisture, heat and rubbing, because wool can felt and shrink. For a valuable wool rug, instead of shop-bought carpet shampoo, the Canadian Conservation Institute advises consulting a professional. More: can a wool rug be washed at home.

Can baking soda and vinegar be used on fitted carpet?

If you use them, only after a test and with a small amount of moisture. Makers such as Shaw warn that too much water without extraction can make fitted carpet wrinkle. For on-site cleaning: fitted carpet cleaning.
