How we choose sources

We take facts from public sources: university extension services, museum conservators, the Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija), veterinary sources, Latvian legislation and rug and fabric manufacturers. Every fact in an article has its source and the date it was checked.

We do not give home recipes with quantities, because the sources differ on them. We write what the sources agree on: blot, do not rub, cold water, no heat, test the product on a hidden spot first. If the advice does not help and the rug has to be sent for cleaning, see the price list.

Frequently asked questions

Does one piece of advice suit all rugs?

No. Wool, viscose, jute and synthetics react differently to water and heat. Before using any product, read the care label and test the product on a hidden spot.

What if I have already tried to clean the stain?

Do not try again with another product. Photograph the stain, remember what you used and write it in the request.
