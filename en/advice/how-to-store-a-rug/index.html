How to wrap and store a rug

Wrapping a rug for storage starts with a clean and completely dry rug: it is then rolled in the direction of the pile onto a wide tube, not folded. Conservators advise covering it with unbleached cotton fabric or acid-free paper and keeping it off the floor, not in a cellar or attic. For a wool rug, moths are a major risk in storage, so cleaning before storage is not a formality.

Wrapping a rug for storage, step by step

  1. Clean it Pest specialists at Washington State University advise putting only items that are properly cleaned and free of pests into storage. Moth larvae feed on wool and avoid light, so a dark storage place suits them.
  2. Dry it completely Roll up only a completely dry rug. Moisture wrapped up with the rug can stay in the packaging, and mould in a porous material often cannot be fully cleaned.
  3. Roll, do not fold Folds leave marks, and threads can break there. Roll in the direction of the pile onto a wide tube.
  4. Cover it Conservators advise unbleached cotton fabric (muslin) or acid-free paper.
  5. Place it properly Keep the rug off the floor, in a dry place and out of direct sunlight. A cellar and an attic are not suitable.
  6. Carry, do not drag Do not lift the rug by one edge and do not drag it across the floor. The same goes for furniture: do not drag it across the rug.

Canadian Conservation Institute, Basic care: carpets and rugs; Washington State University PestSense, Casemaking clothes moth: checked 10.11.2026. US EPA, A Brief Guide to Mold, Moisture and Your Home: checked 07.11.2026.

Plastic film, box or breathable cover

The sources differ. The Canadian Conservation Institute advises a breathable cover: unbleached cotton or acid-free paper. Washington State University mentions airtight containers against moths. Both kinds are used, and in both cases the risk is the same: a damp place and a damp rug.

If you choose plastic film or a closed box, wrap only a completely dry rug and keep it in a dry room.

Moths in storage

Clothes moth larvae feed on wool and other materials of animal origin, and among the items at risk WSU mentions rugs and curtains. The larvae avoid light, so items kept in a dark place are more at risk.

  • Vacuum regularly and clean the places where rugs stand and are stored.
  • Pesticides are not an effective fix. In the long term, finding and removing the source helps.
  • If there are live moth larvae in the rug, it is a pest control job.

Storing rugs in summer

If you take a rug off the floor for several months in summer, the main risks are light and moisture. The Canadian Conservation Institute writes that light dries the fibres and fades the colours, while high humidity damages the fibres and can make the dyes bleed. For comfortable humidity indoors, the Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) cites a relative humidity of 40–60%, and above 80% the risk of mould is high.

If you keep the rug in a room, choose a place out of direct sunlight and with indoor humidity of about 40–60%.

The Health Inspectorate, Health risks, preventive and corrective measures: comfortable humidity 40–60%, above 80% a high risk of mould. Checked 07.11.2026.

What not to do

  • Do not fold a rug for a long time: the fold marks stay, and threads can break.
  • Do not wrap a damp or freshly washed rug.
  • Do not store a rug directly on the floor, in a cellar or in an attic.
  • Do not put a dirty wool rug into storage.

Frequently asked questions

Can a rug be stored in plastic film?

It can, if the rug is clean and completely dry and is kept in a dry place. The Canadian Conservation Institute advises a breathable cover of unbleached cotton or acid-free paper, but airtight packaging is also used against moths.

Can a rug be folded if it is stored for a short time?

It is better to roll it even then. Folds leave marks and threads can break, and this applies to any length of storage.

Does a rug need cleaning before storage?

Yes, especially a wool rug. Pest specialists advise storing only cleaned items free of pests, because moth larvae feed on wool and avoid light.

Can a rug be wrapped straight after cleaning?

Only when it is completely dry, the backing too. A damp rug in plastic film or a box can start to grow mould.
