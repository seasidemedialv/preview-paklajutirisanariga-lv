Greyness from dust usually fades better than yellowing from sun or smoke built up over years. We clean sheers in Riga and the Riga region, from €2.30/m² of fabric.

What makes sheers yellow and grey

Sheers hang right by the glass, so they are hit by both the sun and the dust from an open window. The Canadian Conservation Institute (CCI) writes that light dries the fibres and fades the colours. Yellowing can also come from smoke and kitchen fumes.

A study of tobacco smoke residue (Bahl et al., 2014) found that it stayed in cotton and polyester for 19 months. If people smoke in the room, sheers collect this residue too. More on smell on the page curtain cleaning for smoke and cigarette smell.

Removing yellowing from curtains: what to expect

Not every yellowing can be removed. If it was caused by the sun, the fibre itself has changed, and cleaning usually does not bring it back. If the yellowing is from smoke or the kitchen, it can fade, but we cannot promise perfectly white sheers. Before the job, a specialist looks at your photo and tells you what to expect.

Photograph the sheers in daylight, both the exposed part and a place that was covered by a night curtain or a fold. From the difference between the two places the yellowing can be judged better than from one photo.

Washing sheer curtains at home: why be careful

Chlorine bleach and strong cleaning products can change the colour, and a wrong product can make the dye bleed. So test any product on a hidden spot first and read the label.

NC State Extension’s material on textiles notes that when fabric gets soaked, shrinking and colour change often happen. For long sheers even a small shrinkage means that they no longer reach the floor. If it has already happened, read why curtains shrink after washing.

Pale curtain cleaning: organza, voile and delicate fabrics

Organza and other thin fabrics cost the same as sheers, from €2.30/m². For a delicate fabric the most important things are the fibre composition on the label and whether it has embroidery, beads or metal threads. Write that in the request and add a close-up photo.

Sheers on a rail are usually gathered into folds, so there is much more fabric than the width of the window. Measure the fabric laid flat: width times length.

What usually comes out and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • greyness from dust;
  • fresh stains;
  • yellowing from smoke and the kitchen may fade.

May stay

  • yellowing from the sun;
  • old nicotine yellowing;
  • shrinking after earlier washing;
  • tears and pulled threads in a delicate fabric.

Frequently asked questions

Will the sheers be white after cleaning?

We do not promise that. Greyness from dust usually fades, but yellowing from the sun or from years of smoke may stay.

Can sheers be cleaned on site without taking them down?

Sometimes, if they are only dusty. For yellowing and smell, cleaning with pick-up is usually better. See on-site curtain cleaning.

Why did the sheers get shorter after washing?

When soaked, fabric can shrink. On long sheers this shows at once at the floor. Cleaning does not reverse the shrinking.

Can sheers be washed together with night curtains?

At home you do it at your own risk: a coloured fabric can stain the light one. In the request, write both separately, because their prices differ too.
