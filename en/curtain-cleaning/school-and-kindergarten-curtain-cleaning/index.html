What the hygiene regulations say

For schools, Cabinet Regulation No. 610 requires sun protection in classrooms with south-facing windows, for example curtains or blinds that do not cover the glass when they are not in use. The same regulation requires rooms to be kept clean, the institution to prepare a cleaning plan, and rooms to be wet-cleaned at least once a day (with the exceptions named in the regulation).

Cabinet Regulation No. 610 of 27.12.2002 “Hygiene Requirements for Educational Institutions Implementing the General Basic Education, General Secondary Education, Vocational Basic Education, Industrial Education, or Vocational Secondary Education Programmes”, paragraphs 45, 46 and 47. Checked 10.11.2026.

For kindergartens, Cabinet Regulation No. 890 states that wet cleaning follows the institution’s cleaning and disinfection plan, and that cloakrooms, group and play rooms and toilets are wet-cleaned at least twice a day. Compliance is monitored by the Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija).

Cabinet Regulation No. 890 of 17.09.2013 “Hygiene Requirements for the Providers of Child Supervision Service and Educational Institutions Implementing a Pre-School Education Programme”, paragraph 52. Checked 10.11.2026.

How often curtains are cleaned is decided by the institution in its cleaning plan. If the plan provides for curtain cleaning once a year or in the school holidays, write that in the request so that the work can be arranged for that time.

Classroom curtains and sun protection

In a classroom with south-facing windows, curtains are not only decoration but also sun protection. The sun damages them: light dries the fibres and fades the colours, so on the south side curtains can fade faster and unevenly. Cleaning does not restore the fading.

If the classrooms have Roman or roller blinds, their price depends on the situation: size, mechanism and take-down, fabric. See Roman and roller blind cleaning.

Sports hall and assembly hall curtains

In halls the curtains are large and hang high. The price of take-down and rehanging is decided by the length and height of the rail, the number and weight of the curtains, the type of fixing, and whether steps or scaffolding are needed. See curtain take-down, cleaning and rehanging.

When to plan the work and what to write

  • the number of rooms and the number of curtains in each;
  • the size of one typical curtain, or the length and height of the rail;
  • types of curtains: sheer, heavy, blackout, Roman or roller;
  • whether the curtains must be taken down and rehung;
  • when the rooms are free; if you want the work in the school holidays, give the dates.

If you buy the service through a procurement, a list of the curtains with their sizes means the price can be calculated per m². Kindergartens and schools have a separate page for carpets: kindergarten and school carpet cleaning.

What usually comes out and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • dust and greyness;
  • fresh paint and food stains, if they have not been rubbed;
  • room smell in the fabric may fade.

May stay

  • sun fading in south-facing windows;
  • old and rubbed stains, felt-tip marks and paint;
  • tears and pulled threads;
  • damaged mechanisms and rails: we do not repair those.

Frequently asked questions

Do the regulations say how often curtains must be cleaned in a school?

The regulations require the cleaning of rooms and a cleaning plan for the institution. How often curtains are cleaned is decided by the institution in its plan.

Can the cleaning be done in the school holidays?

In the request, give the dates when the rooms are free. After you fill in the form, a specialist contacts you and gives the price based on the size and the volume.

Can the cleaning be done in parts, class by class?

Write how many rooms can be freed at the same time. The price is calculated by the total fabric area.

How do I measure many identical curtains?

It is enough to measure one typical curtain (fabric width times length) and write how many there are. List the different curtains separately.
