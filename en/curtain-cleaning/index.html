With pick-up or on site: what suits which curtain

With pick-up, curtains are cleaned in a workshop and brought back. This is usually the right option for curtains with stains, smoke smell, lining or pile, and for fabrics whose label does not allow washing in water. On site, the curtains stay on the rail.

Curtain washing and dry cleaning: what the label decides

The care label, not the look of the fabric, tells you whether curtains may be washed in water. Care symbols are described by the GINETEX system and the standard ISO 3758: a crossed-out symbol means “do not”, a circle with a letter indicates professional cleaning, and bars under a symbol mean a gentler process.

Lined curtains have two fabrics, and each may have its own label. Photograph both. Velvet, silk, jacquard and curtains marked for professional cleaning have their own page: curtain dry cleaning.

Sources: GINETEX care symbols brochure, ISO 3758. Checked 07.11.2026.

Why curtains shrink and change colour

NC State Extension (North Carolina State University), in its guide to textiles after a flood, notes that when fabric gets soaked, its colour often changes, the dye bleeds, the fabric shrinks and water marks stay. The fabric maker Brentano writes that natural fibres react to air humidity, and the length of a curtain can change by up to 3%.

Light dries out fibres and fades colours, so on a sunny window a curtain can fade unevenly. Cleaning does not restore fading. If your curtains have already shrunk after washing at home, read why curtains shrink after washing.

How to measure curtains for a price

  1. Measure the fabric, not the window A curtain on a rail is gathered into folds, so the width of the fabric is greater than the rail. If you can, lay the curtain out and measure it stretched.
  2. Each curtain separately Write down the width and length of each curtain in metres. List sheers and night curtains separately, because their prices differ.
  3. If you cannot take them down Measure the length and height of the rail from the floor and add a photo. That is enough to get started.

Mould on curtains

Mould in a porous material often cannot be fully cleaned out. The US Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) writes that mouldy porous materials sometimes have to be thrown away. The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) advises putting textiles covered in mould into a closed bag, and leaving mould that covers more than about 9 m² or is in the building structure to specialists.

If the mould is on the walls or in window reveals, cleaning the curtains does not solve the problem. For treating the room, see room treatment for mould and smell. For marks on a curtain, send a photo: a specialist tells you whether cleaning makes sense.

What to write in the request

  • the type and number of curtains: sheers, night, lined, blackout, Roman or roller;
  • the size of each curtain in metres, or the length and height of the rail;
  • photos of the front and the back, the care label, if there is one;
  • stains, yellowing, smoke smell or damp marks;
  • whether you will take the curtains down yourself, and the type of fixing (hooks, eyelets, tracks);
  • floor, lift and where a car can stop.

What usually comes out and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • dust and greyness;
  • fresh stains that have not been rubbed or washed at home;
  • smoke and kitchen smell may lessen.

May stay

  • sun fading: cleaning does not restore colour;
  • old yellowing on sheers;
  • water marks and shrinkage after earlier washing;
  • cracks or a peeling coating on blackout curtains;
  • smoke smell, if it returns from the room.

Frequently asked questions

Can curtains be washed in a washing machine at home?

Only if the care label allows it. When soaked, the fabric can shrink, change colour or keep water marks. For velvet, lined curtains and blackout curtains the risk is higher, so read the label first.

Can I get the curtains back by a set date?

If you need the curtains by a specific date, write that in the request.

Can curtains and a rug be cleaned in one go?

Yes, write both in one request. Rugs have their own prices: see rug cleaning with pick-up.

Can yellowing be removed from sheers?

Sometimes yellowing lessens, but not every yellowing can be removed, especially if it was caused by sun or smoke. We cannot promise they will turn white. More on the page sheer curtain cleaning.
