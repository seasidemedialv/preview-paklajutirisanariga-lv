Why smoke smell stays in curtains

In a study of tobacco smoke residue in textiles (Bahl et al., 2014), the residue stayed in cotton and polyester for 19 months. An overview in Tobacco Control concluded that ordinary methods, such as cleaning carpets, repainting walls and wiping surfaces, often reduce the smell and the residue only for a time.

Curtains are a large area of fabric hanging at a window, in the path of moving air, so they can take up the smell from the whole room and take it up again after cleaning.

Sources: Bahl et al., 2014 (tobacco residue in textiles); the Tobacco Control overview on thirdhand tobacco smoke. Checked 07.11.2026.

If the whole room smells

If the smell is in the walls, the ceiling, the soft furnishings and the carpets, cleaned curtains will soon take it up again. Then curtain cleaning is only part of the work. For treating the room, see room smell treatment. If smoking continues in the room, the smell will come back in any case.

Nicotine yellowing on sheer curtains

Light sheer curtains in a smoky room can turn yellowish. This yellowing can fade with cleaning, but not every yellowing can be removed, and we do not promise whiteness. More on the page sheer curtain cleaning.

Curtain cleaning after a fire

After a fire, or smoke from a neighbour’s flat, the smell and the soot are all over the room. Curtains are only one of the things that have taken them up. Send a close-up photo of the curtains and write what happened; before the job, a specialist tells you what to expect. If the fabric is scorched or melted, cleaning does not repair it.

Cleaning curtains for grease and kitchen smell

Kitchen curtains take up fumes from the hob, and they can turn yellowish. If the curtains hang close to the hob, write that in the request and add a photo of the bottom and the edges of the fabric.

With pick-up or on site

For curtains with a smoke smell, cleaning with pick-up is usually the better choice: the curtains are cleaned in a workshop and brought back. While the curtains are away, there is time to air the room and clean other surfaces. If it is hard to take the curtains down yourself, see curtain take-down, cleaning and rehanging; it is priced case by case.

What to do before the request

  • Write whether only the curtains smell or the whole room.
  • Photograph the curtains and the label, and measure the width and length of each curtain.
  • Do not mask the smell with perfume or an air freshener: then it is harder to tell how strong it is.
  • If there are carpets that smell too, write about them in the same request.

What usually comes out and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • smoke and kitchen smell may fade;
  • dust and greyness;
  • part of the yellowing on sheers.

May stay

  • smell, if it comes back from the room or if smoking continues in the room;
  • old nicotine yellowing;
  • burns and melted fibre after a fire;
  • soot marks that have soaked into the fabric.

Frequently asked questions

Will the curtains no longer smell of smoke after cleaning?

The smell can be reduced, but we cannot promise that it will go. If the walls and furniture in the room smell or smoking continues, the smell will come back.

Can curtains with a smoke smell be cleaned on site?

For a strong smoke smell, curtains are usually best cleaned with pick-up; before the job, a specialist tells you what to expect. If the smell is light and the curtains hang high, see on-site curtain cleaning.

Do the carpets need cleaning too?

If the carpets smell too, yes: otherwise the curtains can take up the smell again. For carpets, see carpet odour treatment.

Can curtains be cleaned after a fire?

It depends on the fabric and the damage. Send photos: cleaning does not repair scorched or melted fabric.
