Blackout curtain cleaning

Blackout curtain cleaning differs from ordinary curtain cleaning in one thing: in many blackout curtains the light is blocked by a coating on the back of the fabric, and this coating is sensitive to heat.

Why a blackout curtain is different

For an ordinary curtain, the fabric and the colour matter. For a blackout curtain with a coating, what is applied to the back matters too. The coating is not a fibre, so it reacts differently to heat and to hot or aggressive washing.

Photograph the curtain from both sides. From the back you can see whether there is a coating and whether it already has cracks or peeled patches. If the curtain is double, with a separate blackout lining, write that in the request.

What heat does to the coating

Makers of blackout fabrics warn that heat, such as a tumble dryer or a hot iron on the coated side, and also hot or aggressive washing can make the coating crack or peel off. That is why the label decides, and its temperature instruction must not be exceeded.

Source: Wolf-Gordon, Eclipse blackout fabric care page (maker’s instructions). Checked 10.11.2026.

Cracks in the coating can be seen as points or lines of light when the curtain is drawn in the daytime. Cleaning does not patch them.

Washing blackout curtains at home: what not to do

  • Do not dry them in a tumble dryer.
  • Do not iron the coated side with a hot iron.
  • Do not wash hotter than the label allows, and do not choose an intensive programme.
  • Do not use chlorine bleach or strong products: they can change the colour.
  • Do not steam them on the rail: steam is heat too.

If there is no label or it is unreadable, it is better not to wash the curtain, and to send a photo.

Blackout roller blinds and double curtains

Blackout fabric is also used in roller blinds. There the fabric is attached to a tube and a mechanism, so the price depends on the situation: size, mechanism, take-down and fabric. More on the page Roman and roller blind cleaning.

In a double curtain the blackout lining is sewn in or attached to the decorative fabric. The front fabric and the lining can be made of different materials, so photograph both labels. If the lining is a separate curtain on its own rail, it is measured and cleaned as a separate curtain.

What to write in the request

  • the number of curtains and the size of each in metres (fabric width times length);
  • photos of the front, the back and the label;
  • whether the coating already has cracks or peeled patches;
  • whether the curtains must be taken down and what the fixing is (hooks, eyelets, tracks);
  • the floor, whether there is a lift, and where a car can stop.

What usually comes out and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • dust and greyness on the surface;
  • fresh stains that have not been rubbed;
  • smell in the fabric may fade.

May stay

  • a cracked or peeled coating;
  • points of light that are already visible;
  • sun fading on the surface;
  • fold lines where the coating is already damaged.

Frequently asked questions

Can blackout curtains be washed in a washing machine?

Only if the label allows it, and without exceeding its temperature. Makers warn that hot or aggressive washing and a tumble dryer can damage the coating.

Why does a blackout curtain let light through after washing?

Most likely the coating has cracked or peeled off. Cleaning does not repair it, and we do not do sewing or repair work.

Can blackout curtains be cleaned on site?

That depends on the label, the fabric and how dirty it is. More on the page on-site curtain cleaning.

Can a blackout curtain be ironed?

Makers warn about a hot iron on the coated side. See the label: if ironing is allowed, iron only the front and only at the stated temperature.

Are the blackout lining and the face fabric charged separately?

No, if the lining is sewn in or attached: a double curtain is priced by the fabric area, as for heavy curtains, from €3.70/m². If the lining is a separate curtain on its own rail, write the sizes of both.
