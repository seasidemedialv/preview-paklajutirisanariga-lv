The price depends on the area and the volume, and the unit prices are the same: from €2.30/m² for sheer curtains, from €3.70/m² of fabric for heavy curtains. We work in Riga and the Riga region; in the request, write the rooms, the number of curtains and the sizes.

What to write in the request

  • rooms and the number of curtains in each: guest rooms, a hall, a meeting room, a stage;
  • types of curtains: sheer, night, blackout, Roman or roller;
  • the size of one typical curtain (width times length) or the length and height of the rail;
  • whether the curtains must be taken down and rehung;
  • when the rooms are free and how many rooms can be freed at the same time;
  • stains, yellowing, smoke or kitchen smell.

With this information the price can be calculated by m² even before the job. A photo of the curtains at one typical window and a photo of the curtain label help more than a long description.

Hotel room curtains

A room often has two layers: sheer curtains and a blackout curtain. Each has its own price, so list them separately. For blackout curtains with a coating, makers warn that heat can make the coating crack; more on the page blackout curtain cleaning.

If the rooms can be freed only by floors or groups, write that in the request. Hotels have a separate page for carpets: hotel carpet cleaning.

Restaurant and café curtains

In a restaurant, curtains collect kitchen fumes and smell. Yellowing from the kitchen or from smoke cannot always be cleaned off. In a study of tobacco smoke residue (Bahl et al., 2014), it stayed in cotton and polyester for 19 months. If the smell is all over the hall, curtain cleaning alone does not solve it.

More on smell on the page curtain cleaning for smoke and cigarette smell.

Office curtains

Offices often have Roman and roller blinds or fabric blinds; they are priced case by case (size, mechanism and take-down, fabric), see Roman and roller blind cleaning. On the sunny side the fabric fades: light dries the fibres and fades the colours, and cleaning does not restore the colour.

Stage, theatre and community centre curtains

Stage curtains are large, heavy and hang high. The price of take-down and rehanging is decided by the length and height of the rail, the number and weight of the curtains, the type of fixing, and whether a ladder or scaffolding is needed. That is why such a job needs photos and sizes; see curtain take-down, cleaning and rehanging.

Fire-retardant treatment is not part of this service. If the fabric has such a treatment, write that in the request. For auditorium carpets, see carpet cleaning in a theatre, concert hall and church.

Price for businesses

For businesses the price depends on the area and the volume; the unit prices are the same “from” prices. Thin and day curtains, sheers: from €2.30/m². Heavy, night and double curtains: from €3.70/m². Roman and roller blinds, and also take-down and rehanging: the price depends on the job.

Example: 20 heavy curtains, each 1.5 × 2.6 m of fabric: 78 m² × €3.70 = from €288.60. Pick-up and return in Riga costs €10 (both ways together); in the Riga region up to about 15 km from the Riga boundary it is also €10, and further out another €10 is added. What usually comes out and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • dust and greyness;
  • fresh stains;
  • kitchen and smoke smell may fade.

May stay

  • sun fading on the sunny side;
  • old yellowing from the kitchen or smoke;
  • cracks in the blackout coating;
  • smell that comes back from the room.

Frequently asked questions

Can the cleaning be done in stages so that the hotel keeps working?

Write in the request how many rooms or halls can be freed at the same time and when. After you fill in the form, a specialist contacts you and gives the price based on the size, the fibre and the photos.

How is the price calculated for a large number of curtains?

For businesses the price depends on the area and the volume, and the unit prices are the same “from” prices. To work out the total, we need a list of the curtains with sizes; if the rooms are identical, the curtain sizes of one room and the number of rooms are enough.

Do you restore the fire-retardant treatment on stage curtains?

No, fire-retardant treatment is not part of this service. If the curtain has such a treatment, write that in the request.

Can carpets be cleaned at the same site too?

Yes, write both in one request. For carpets, see carpet cleaning for businesses and institutions.
