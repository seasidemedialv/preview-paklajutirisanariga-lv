What is different about a Roman or roller blind

A Roman blind is a flat fabric with rods across it, and cords raise it into even folds. The rods are usually in pockets on the back of the fabric, and the cords run through rings to the mechanism at the top. In a roller blind the fabric is wound on a tube, which can be in a cassette, and it has a weight bar at the bottom.

The mechanism is not a textile, and it cannot be cleaned like fabric. So the first question is whether the fabric can be separated from the mechanism and how easy that is.

Why the price depends on the situation

  • Size: the width and height of the fabric when the blind is lowered.
  • Mechanism and take-down: whether the fabric can be separated from the rods, cords or tube, and whether the blind must be taken down from the window frame or the wall.
  • Fabric: fibre, lining, blackout coating.

From photos and sizes, a specialist gives the price. Add photos from the front, from the back and a close-up of the top mechanism.

Cleaning and washing Roman blinds

If the rods can be pulled out of the pockets and the cords disconnected, the fabric is afterwards similar to an ordinary curtain, and its label applies. If the rods are sewn in and the cords are attached to the fabric, this must be known before the job. If a Roman blind has a lining or is made of two fabrics, photograph the back and both labels, if there are any.

At home, do not wash a Roman blind with rods in a washing machine: the rods can bend and damage the fabric. If there is a stain on the fabric, blot it with a clean cloth and do not rub.

Roller blind cleaning

The fabric of a roller blind is attached to the tube and the bottom bar. If the fabric has a blackout coating, the same heat risk applies as for blackout curtains: makers warn that heat can make the coating crack or peel off. More on the page blackout curtain cleaning.

In the request, write whether the roller blind is in a cassette, whether it is fixed to the window frame, and whether the mechanism has a chain or a motor.

Vertical and fabric blind cleaning

Fabric blinds and vertical blinds consist of separate fabric slats hanging on a track. Their price also depends on the situation. Write the number and length of the slats and add a photo. The price is set from the photo and the number of slats.

Plastic and metal blinds are not textiless, and this page is not about them.

What usually comes out and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • dust on the fabric;
  • fresh stains that have not been rubbed;
  • greyness on a light fabric.

May stay

  • bent rods, broken cords or a damaged mechanism: we do not repair those;
  • sun fading;
  • cracks in the blackout coating;
  • water marks after earlier washing.

Frequently asked questions

Why is there no price per m² for Roman blinds?

For each Roman and roller blind the mechanism and the take-down are different. The price is set from the size, the mechanism and the fabric, once a photo can be seen.

Can a Roman blind be washed in a washing machine together with the rods?

No. The rods and cords must be taken out before washing. If they are sewn in, washing at home is not advised, and it is better to send a photo.

Do you clean aluminium or plastic blinds?

No, aluminium and plastic blinds are not textiles, and we do not clean them.

Do I have to take the roller blind down myself?

Not necessarily. Write how it is fixed and add a photo: take-down is one of the price factors.
