About us

Paklāju Tīrīšana Rīgā cleans rugs, fitted carpets and curtains in Riga and the Riga region. We clean fitted carpets and large synthetic rugs at your home, while rugs with pick-up are cleaned in a workshop and brought back. Prices are published, and we take requests through a form.

What we do

  • Rug cleaning at home: large synthetic rugs on site.
  • Rug cleaning with pick-up: rugs that can be rolled up, in a workshop.
  • Fitted carpet cleaning: soft floor covering in flats, stairwells and offices.
  • Professional rug cleaning: wool, silk, viscose, hide, jute and hand-made rugs.
  • Carpet stain removal: stains, urine and smell.
  • Commercial carpet cleaning: offices, hotels, restaurants, schools.
  • Curtain cleaning: thin and heavy curtains, sheers.

All services with descriptions: services.

What usually comes out and what may stay

A fresh stain usually comes out better than an old one. Paint, hair dye and bleach marks often stay. A smell can be reduced, but we cannot promise it will disappear. Flattened viscose pile may stay. We do not quote percentages, we do not promise drying times and we make no promises about health.

Before the job, a specialist tells you what to expect from your rug, and then you decide.

How the price is formed

The prices are published on the page prices as “from” prices (per m² for rugs, per m² of fabric for curtains). For jute, sisal and coir rugs the price is given from a photo; for Roman blinds and roller blinds and for curtain take-down and rehanging the price depends on the situation. The prices depend on the size, the fibre, the pile length and thickness, the dirt and stains, smell or urine, access and distance. The minimum order is €45 (for the cleaning only): if the total is lower, you pay €45.

Why only a form

The price of a rug is set by details: the size, the fibre, the stains and the address. Photos and these details in the request help the specialist give a more exact price. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes. The form is on the page contact.

What we do not do

  • We do not repair rugs or restore fringes and edges.
  • We do not store rugs.
  • We do not clean rental (exchange) entrance mats and car mats.
  • We do not clean sofas, mattresses or chairs.

Languages

The site is in Latvian, English and Russian. You can write the request in any of these languages.

Frequently asked questions

Does a request commit me to anything?

No. A request only asks us to get in touch and name a price. You can decline.

Do you also clean carpets for businesses?

Yes. For businesses the price depends on the area and the volume; the unit prices are the same “from” prices. More: commercial carpet cleaning.
