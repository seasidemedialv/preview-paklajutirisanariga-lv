What is left in a carpet after renovation

After renovation, there are usually three kinds of dirt in a carpet. First, fine plaster and cement dust that settles in the pile and the backing. Second, drops of paint, varnish and glue. Third, sand and building debris brought in on shoes and tools. Each comes out differently, so say in the request exactly what is on the carpet.

If the carpet was covered during the renovation, often only the path from the door to the work area gets dirty. If it was not covered, the dust is over the whole area, including under the furniture.

What to do before cleaning and what not to do

  1. Wait until the work is finished Cleaning makes sense once the dusty work in the room is finished; otherwise the carpet will be dusty again.
  2. Collect the debris and vacuum the dust Collect building debris by hand or with a brush, then vacuum the carpet several times.
  3. Do not rub fresh paint Rubbing pushes the stain deeper. Blot fresh liquid with a clean cloth.
  4. Do not use solvents Solvents can damage the carpet backing, and chlorine bleach and strong products change the colour. Test any product on a hidden spot first.

Sources: New Mexico State University (NMSU G-402) and the University of Georgia extension service on products and stains; Canadian Conservation Institute on blotting. Checked 07.11.2026 and 10.11.2026.

If the fitted carpet is new

Sometimes a new carpet is fitted during a renovation. The carpet maker Fibreworks writes that a new carpet with a latex backing may smell of ammonia at first. Ventilate the room first. If the carpet has the maker’s care rules, add them to the request.

Dust under furniture and along the walls

If furniture stood on the carpet or was moved during the renovation, plaster and cement dust can also be under it and along the walls. Before cleaning, remove from the carpet anything that can be lifted, and mention heavy furniture in the request.

Photos of these places help to give the price, because dirt and stains affect it. If there is still renovation dust on the window sills and shelves in the room, wipe it off first, or it will fall onto the cleaned carpet.

What usually comes out and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • dust and sand in the pile;
  • a grey path from the door to the work area;
  • fresh stains, if they have not been rubbed.

May stay

  • dried paint and varnish;
  • marks from bleach and strong products;
  • backing damage from solvents;
  • burns, cuts and tears: we do not do repairs.

A fresh stain usually comes out better than an old one.

Frequently asked questions

Can a paint stain be removed from a carpet?

Not always. Paint often stays, especially if it has dried or been rubbed. Send a close-up photo of the stain and write what kind of paint it was, if you know.

When is it better to clean a carpet after a renovation?

When all the dusty work is finished. Before that, collect the building debris and vacuum the dust.

The new fitted carpet smells. Does it need cleaning?

Not necessarily. The carpet maker writes that a new carpet with a latex backing may smell of ammonia at first. Ventilate the room first.

Do you also clean floors and windows after a renovation?

No, we clean rugs, fitted carpet and curtains. If the curtains have become dusty during the renovation, see curtain cleaning and washing.
