At home or with pick-up?

A simple rule: a fitted carpet, which cannot be lifted, and large synthetic rugs are cleaned at home, while a rug of natural or delicate fibre that can be rolled up is cleaned in a workshop. A fitted carpet (carpeting) is fixed to the floor, so it is cleaned on site only. Wool, silk, viscose and hand-made rugs need controlled wet cleaning and full drying, which a living room floor cannot provide. For a hide rug, water is a risk.

The form has a “Where to clean?” field. If you know what suits you, choose it. If you do not, tick that you are not sure and add photos of the rug from the top and from the underside: a specialist will recommend the most suitable option from the photos. A small synthetic rug can also be cleaned with pick-up if you prefer.

Can you clean a rug yourself?

Part of the work you can do yourself. Day to day, vacuum the rug regularly, also under furniture and in dark corners. If something spills, act at once: blot the liquid with a clean cloth and do not rub, because rubbing pushes the stain deeper. For a blood stain use cold water, never hot, because heat sets it. Before using any product, read the care label and test the product on a hidden spot.

Mistakes to avoid at home. Hot water together with rubbing, or the movement of a washing machine, makes wool felt, and the rug may shrink; this usually cannot be reversed. Soaking in the bath harms viscose, which becomes much weaker when wet, and jute, which can turn brown in water. It also harms tufted rugs, because strong wetting and heat can weaken the glue in their backing. The Canadian Conservation Institute does not advise using shop-bought carpet shampoos on valuable rugs, because afterwards dirt sticks to the fibres more easily.

If a stain does not come out or the rug is made of wool, silk or viscose, send a photo and a specialist gives the price. Also read: how to remove a stain from a rug and can a rug be washed in a washing machine. More articles with sources: advice.

Frequently asked questions

How soon do you get in touch after a request?

After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes. Then a specialist gives the price based on the size, the fibre and the photos.

Will you remove any stain?

We do not promise that. A fresh stain usually comes out better than an old one. Paint, hair dye and bleach marks often stay. From the photo, a specialist tells you what to expect.

How do I find out what fibre my rug is made of?

Look for a care label on the underside or the edge of the rug. If there is none, photograph the rug from the top and from the underside and, in the “Where to clean?” field of the form, tick that you are not sure. A specialist will recommend the most suitable option from the photos.

Is the pick-up charge included in the minimum order?

No. The minimum order of €45 applies to the cleaning only, and the €10 pick-up and return charge is added. If cleaning a small rug with pick-up in Riga comes to €22, you pay €45 + €10 = €55.

When can I use the rug at home after cleaning?

How long a rug takes to dry depends on the fibre, the pile thickness, the humidity of the room air and ventilation. More: how long a rug takes to dry after cleaning.

Do you also clean sofas and mattresses?

No, we clean rugs, fitted carpets and curtains. For sofa, mattress and chair cleaning see mebelutirisanariga.lv.
