What the price depends on

  • The size in m² (of the rug or of the curtain fabric).
  • The fibre: wool, cotton, silk, viscose, hide, hand-made and jute rugs need a workshop.
  • Pile length and thickness.
  • Dirt and stains.
  • Smell or urine: treatment extra from €3/m².
  • Access: floor, lift and a place where a car can stop.
  • Distance from the Riga boundary.

The prices say “from” because two rugs of the same size can need different work. Send a photo and a specialist gives the price. More by service: rug cleaning at home, rug cleaning with pick-up, fitted carpet cleaning and carpet stain removal.

Minimum price for carpet cleaning: €45

The minimum order is €45: if the total is lower, you pay €45. The minimum applies to the cleaning only; the €10 pick-up and return charge and the travel charge are added. If you have several rugs to clean, or curtains as well, write them in one request.

Carpet cleaning price with pick-up and travel

Pick-up and return in Riga costs €10 (both ways together); in the Riga region up to about 15 km from the Riga boundary it is also €10, and further out another €10 is added. For example, pick-up and return in Ogre or Sigulda costs €10 + €10 = €20.

Travel is included in Riga and up to about 15 km outside the Riga boundary; further out it is from €10. It is included, for example, in Mārupe, Salaspils and Ķekava, while in Ogre and Jelgava it is from €10. In Jūrmala it depends on the address: Bulduri is about 11 km from the Riga boundary, Sloka and Ķemeri are further than 15 km. In Bulduri travel is included; in Sloka and Ķemeri it is from €10. How to measure a rug

Measure the length and width of the rug in metres and multiply: a 2 × 3 m rug is 6 m². Do not count the fringe; measure only the rug itself.

For a round rug, measure the diameter and enter it in the form. The price of a round or oval rug is set by the rectangle around it: a round rug 2 m across is 2 × 2 m = 4 m².

For a fitted carpet, give the area of the room in m². For curtains, measure the fabric itself, width × length, not the size of the window.

Curtain cleaning price per m²

From €2.30/m² for thin curtains and sheers, from €3.70/m² for heavy ones, per square metre of fabric, not per window. For Roman blinds and roller blinds, and for take-down with rehanging, the price depends on the situation. More: curtain cleaning.

Prices for businesses

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to clean a round rug?

The price of a round rug is set by the rectangle around it. A round synthetic rug 2 m across, at home: 4 m² × €6 = €24, below the minimum, so you pay €45.

Is the €10 for pick-up charged each way?

No, €10 in Riga is for pick-up and return together. In the Riga region up to about 15 km from the Riga boundary it is also €10, and further out another €10 is added.

Why is the price for a silk rug given only from a photo?

The work depends on the fibre, the backing and the condition; a specialist can see part of that in a photo. Add photos of the face and the back.
