Why a large rug is cleaned on the floor

A rug of 3 × 4 m or bigger is heavy, and carrying it rolled up through a narrow stairwell or in a lift is hard. A table, sofa or bed may stand on it. If the fibre is synthetic and tolerates moisture, it is simpler to clean the rug where it lies than to lift it out of the room.

If a rug does have to be moved, do not lift it by one edge and do not drag furniture across it. This is what conservators advise.

What is different about a large rug compared with a small one

On a large rug the dirt is uneven. The path from the door to the sofa is grey, while under the bed or the wardrobe the rug is almost clean. The spot by the window may be paler, because light dries the fibres and bleaches the dyes. Conservators advise turning rugs from time to time so that they wear evenly.

A large area has more moisture to dry. How long a rug takes to dry depends on the fibre, the pile thickness, the backing, the room temperature, the humidity and the ventilation. If the rug is thick or long-pile, see also shaggy rug cleaning.

Source: Canadian Conservation Institute, care of carpets and rugs (light, turning, moving). Checked 10.11.2026.

When a large rug is cleaned in a workshop after all

Size does not change the nature of the fibre. Wool, cotton, silk, viscose, hand-made and Persian rugs are cleaned only with pick-up, even if they are large. Hot water and rubbing can make wool felt and shrink, and these fibres need controlled wet cleaning and full drying, which a living room floor cannot give. For these rugs, see rug cleaning with pick-up and return.

A large 3 × 4 m wool rug: 12 m² × €10 = from €120 + pick-up and return €10 = from €130.

How to measure a large rug and what to write

  1. Measure Length × width in metres, without the fringe. For a round rug, measure the diameter: it is priced by the rectangle around it, so 2 m across is 4 m².
  2. Photograph The whole rug from above, the path and the stains up close, and one corner from the underside.
  3. Write about the furniture Take chairs, small tables and plant pots off the rug. Mention heavy furniture in the request.
  4. Write about access The floor, whether there is a lift and where a car can stop.

What usually comes out and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • dust and sand in the pile;
  • a grey path where people walk the most;
  • fresh stains, if they have not been rubbed.

May stay

  • a paler spot by the window, faded by the sun;
  • worn and flattened pile on the path;
  • marks from furniture legs;
  • old stains, paint and bleach patches.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a large rug?

Large rug cleaning applies to rugs from 3 × 4 m (about 12 m²) and bigger. The price is calculated per m², so write the length and width in the request.

Can a large synthetic rug be cleaned in a workshop too?

Yes, if that is more convenient for you: a rug with pick-up, cleaning in a workshop and return (synthetic) costs from €10/m², and pick-up and return in Riga costs €10.

Will the rug be an even colour after cleaning?

Not always. If part of the rug has faded from light or worn on the path, cleaning removes the dirt but does not restore the colour or the pile.

Do I need to clear the whole room?

Take chairs, small tables and plant pots off the rug. If a sofa, bed or wardrobe stands on the rug, mention it in the request and add a photo.
