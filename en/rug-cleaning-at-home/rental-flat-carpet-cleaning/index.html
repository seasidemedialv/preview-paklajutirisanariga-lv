Carpet cleaning after tenants

After tenants, a carpet often shows three things: paths where people walked the most, drink and food stains, and sometimes a smell from animals or smoking. Each has a different outcome, so write in the request what exactly has happened and add photos.

Animal urine smell can cover a larger area than the visible stain: feline veterinarians write that it can be up to three times larger. If the urine has reached the backing or the underlay, the smell can return. More on the page carpet urine cleaning.

Tobacco smoke residue stays in textiles for a long time. In one study it was still found in cotton and polyester after 19 months, and an overview of studies notes that cleaning carpets, painting and wiping walls usually gives only a short-term result. Smoke smell can be reduced, but it may return from the room. More: carpet odour treatment.

Sources: Feline Veterinary Medical Association on the area of urine smell; Bahl et al., 2014, and the Tobacco Control overview of smoke residue in textiles. Checked 07.11.2026.

Carpet cleaning before moving out

If the flat has to be handed over, first read what your tenancy agreement says about carpets and cleaning and, if needed, ask the landlord. We do not judge the tenancy terms; we clean carpets. Before cleaning, photograph any damage, such as burns, tears and worn areas, because cleaning does not remove them.

Do not plan the cleaning for the last moment. How long a rug takes to dry depends on the fibre, the pile thickness, the backing, the room humidity and the ventilation, and a damp rug is hard to assess.

Carpet cleaning before letting and for short-term lets

Before new tenants, it is better to clean the carpet while the flat is empty. In short-term lets, including Airbnb flats, where guests change often, it is important to blot a fresh stain at once with a clean cloth and not rub.

In the request, say when the flat is free. If you have several flats, list them all in one request, giving the rugs and sizes for each.

What to write in the request

  1. Rugs The size of each rug in metres, or the area of the fitted carpet in m², and the fibre if you know it.
  2. Photos The whole room, the stains and damage up close, one corner of a rug from the underside.
  3. What has happened Stains, animals, smoking, and what has already been tried.
  4. Access and time The floor, the lift, where a car can stop and when the flat is free.

Frequently asked questions

Will the landlord return the deposit after the carpet is cleaned?

That is decided by your tenancy agreement and the landlord, not by the carpet cleaning. We can clean the carpet, but cleaning does not remove damage, burns and wear.

Will cigarette smoke smell go away from the carpet?

We cannot promise that. Smoke residue stays in textiles for a long time, and the smell can return from the walls and other things in the room. Cleaning the carpet can reduce it.

Can a sofa and a mattress in a rental flat be cleaned too?

We do not clean sofas and mattresses. For cleaning sofas, mattresses and chairs, see mebelutirisanariga.lv.

Can the request be sent by the tenant instead of the owner?

Yes. The request can be sent by anyone who can arrange access to the flat and payment.
