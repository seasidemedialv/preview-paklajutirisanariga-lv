A fresh stain on the surface usually responds better than an old one. If the urine has reached the backing, the underlay or the floor, the smell may come back even after cleaning, and a specialist tells you this before the job.

Why urine in a carpet is a different job from an ordinary stain

Urine often soaks down to the backing and, if the rug is thin, also to the floor or the underlay beneath it. The Feline Veterinary Medical Association (Feline VMA) writes that the area of smell can be up to three times bigger than the visible stain. So what you see on the surface is often not all of it.

With a fitted carpet, urine can get into the underlay and the floor, which cannot be lifted. With a rug that lies on the floor, you can look at the underside and at the floor under it. Before you send a request, lift a corner of the rug and see whether there is a dark area on the backing and whether the floor smells too.

Cat urine in a carpet

Cats often return to the same place. International Cat Care writes that heavily soiled material stays attractive for repeat marking even after cleaning, and if possible it should be thrown away. If a cat uses the rug again and again, the effect of cleaning may be short-lived until the cause is dealt with.

The MSD Veterinary Manual advises that in cases of repeat marking, health causes should first be ruled out with a veterinarian. Cleaning is the second step.

Dog urine in a carpet

The same first-aid rules apply to dog urine (see below): blot, no heat, no ammonia products. If there have been accidents in several places, photograph each one, because the price is calculated for the treated area. If the rug is wool or hand-made, urine is also a colour question: for such a rug the colourfastness must be tested on a hidden spot before it is wetted, because the dyes can bleed. We clean such rugs with pick-up in a workshop; more on the page wool rug cleaning.

What to do until the rug is cleaned

  1. Blot Soak up the urine with a clean cloth or paper, pressing rather than rubbing.
  2. Do not use ammonia Feline VMA warns that products containing ammonia smell like urine to a cat and can attract it back.
  3. No heat Do not use hot water or a hair dryer. On wool, hot water together with rubbing can cause shrinking.
  4. Test the product ASPCA mentions enzyme products for pet urine. Test any product on a hidden spot first.
  5. Write it down Which animal, how long ago, how many times and what you have already used. All of this is useful in the request.

Sources: Feline VMA, House Soiling handout; ASPCA, Urine marking in cats; MSD Veterinary Manual; International Cat Care. Checked 07.11.2026.

If the whole room smells

If the urine is also on the floor, the walls or the furniture and the smell comes from the room, not only from the carpet, carpet cleaning will not solve the problem. Then see: smell treatment for the whole room.

What to expect after urine treatment

Usually comes out

  • a fresh urine stain on the surface, if it has not been rubbed;
  • a light smell in the pile can fade if the cause has been dealt with;
  • a single accident in a synthetic rug.

May stay

  • smell, if the urine has reached the backing, the underlay or the floor;
  • a yellow or lighter spot where the urine has changed the colour;
  • a place the animal returns to again and again;
  • an underlay that may need to be replaced.

A smell can be reduced, but we cannot promise that it will go. The smell may come back, especially if the urine has reached the backing.

Frequently asked questions

Will the cat no longer urinate on the rug after cleaning?

We cannot promise that. Heavily soiled material can stay attractive to a cat even after cleaning, and repeat marking is a question for a veterinarian.

How do I tell whether the urine has reached the floor?

Lift a corner of the rug. A dark area on the backing or a smell from the floor under the rug means that the urine has gone through. Photograph it and add it to the request.

Do I pay for urine treatment on the whole rug?

Treatment costs from €3/m² for the treated area, and the cleaning is priced by the size of the whole rug.

Can a urine stain on a wool rug be cleaned at home?

We clean wool rugs with pick-up in a workshop. At home, only blot the urine and do not use hot water and do not rub.
