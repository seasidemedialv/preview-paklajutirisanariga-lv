How hair differs from ordinary cleaning

Hair catches in the pile, and the longer and thicker the pile, the deeper it stays. For a long-pile (shaggy) rug this is a question of price: a shaggy or thick rug costs from €10/m², not from €6/m² like a synthetic short-pile rug. In a short, smooth pile the hair stays closer to the surface.

The second difference is that a pet usually means other things too. Say in the request whether the rug has urine, vomit stains, a smell or scratched fringe, because that changes both the price and what you can expect.

Rug cleaning at home with pets

Before cleaning at home, it is better to take the animal to another room so that it does not run over a wet rug. Take the bowls, the bed, chairs, small tables and plant pots off the rug. Mention heavy furniture on the rug in the request. After cleaning, let the rug dry before the animal lies on it again. How long a rug takes to dry depends on the fibre, the thickness of the pile, the humidity of the room air and the ventilation.

A wool rug and pets

We clean wool rugs with pick-up, in a workshop. Hot water together with rubbing can make wool felt, and the rug can shrink, so do not use anything hot on wool at home. If a cat or a dog has urinated on a wool rug, the colourfastness must be tested on a hidden spot before the rug is wetted. More: wool rug cleaning.

What to do between cleanings

  • Vacuum the rug regularly: the less hair builds up, the less of it is pressed into the pile.
  • Blot fresh urine or vomit at once with a clean cloth instead of rubbing.
  • Do not use products that contain ammonia: feline veterinarians warn that to a cat they smell like urine.

Fleas and allergy are not a rug cleaning job

If there are fleas in the rug or on the animal, it is a pest control job: flea and other insect pest control. Rug cleaning reduces the dust and hair in the rug, but it is not a treatment for allergy. If someone in the family is allergic, see carpet cleaning for allergy sufferers.

What usually comes out and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • hair and dust in short pile;
  • dirt from paws and greyness;
  • a fresh accident that has been blotted and not rubbed.

May stay

  • part of the hair deep in long pile;
  • urine smell, if the urine has reached the backing or the floor;
  • scratched or chewed fringe: we do not do repairs or fringe restoration;
  • colour changes where the animal often lies or urinates.

Frequently asked questions

Will any hair be left in the rug after cleaning?

We do not promise that. In short pile the hair usually comes out better, but some hair deep in long, thick pile may stay. A photo helps to assess the pile before the order.

Does the whole rug need cleaning if the cat urinated in only one place?

The cleaning is priced by the size of the rug, but urine treatment is charged by the treated area, from €3/m². Say how many spots there are and add a photo.

Can you also clean a sofa where the dog sleeps?

We clean rugs, fitted carpets and curtains. For cleaning sofas, mattresses and chairs, see mebelutirisanariga.lv.

Can hair be vacuumed out of a shaggy rug?

In short pile, regular vacuuming helps. In long, thick pile part of the hair stays deep, which is why the price for a shaggy rug is from €10/m².
