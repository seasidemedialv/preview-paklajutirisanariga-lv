Tobacco and smoke smell in a carpet

Tobacco smoke residue stays in textiles for a long time. In one study it was still found in cotton and polyester after 19 months, and an overview of smokers' homes concludes that carpet cleaning, painting and washing surfaces give only a short-term result. This means that if smoking continues in the room, the smell may come back from the room. If a flat or a house was bought from a smoker, the carpet is only one of the places where the smell stays.

Sources: Bahl et al., 2014, on tobacco residue in cotton and polyester; an overview in Tobacco Control on cleaning smokers' homes. Checked 07.11.2026.

Carpet cleaning after a fire

After a fire, smoke gets not only into the carpet but also into the walls, ceilings, furniture and curtains. The smoke smell in a carpet can be reduced, but it can come back from the room if the rest of the room has not been dealt with. If the whole house smells, see first: room smell treatment. After that the carpet can be cleaned. Smoke residue also stays in curtains; for them, see the page curtain cleaning for smoke and cigarette smell. Photograph any damage before cleaning.

Damp smell and mould on a carpet

The Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija) writes that in a room with relative air humidity above 80% the risk of mould is high, and the comfort level is 40–60%. A carpet that lies on a cold or damp floor or has stood in a cellar for a long time can start to smell from the backing. The US Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) writes that porous materials in which mould has set cannot always be cleaned completely, and may have to be thrown away.

If mould spots are visible on the carpet, the Health Inspectorate advises putting mouldy textiles in a sealed bag. Mould on the walls and in the room is not a carpet cleaning job. If the carpet was flooded, see carpet cleaning after a flood.

Sources: Health Inspectorate, Health risks, preventive and corrective measures; US EPA, A Brief Guide to Mold, Moisture and Your Home. Checked 07.11.2026.

Latex backing smell

In a tufted rug the yarn is pushed into the backing fabric and fixed with latex glue. Fibreworks writes that a new rug with a latex backing may at first smell of ammonia, while an old backing may in time start to crumble and smell by itself. In the second case the smell comes from the material itself, and cleaning may not change it. More about such rugs: tufted rug cleaning.

What to do before cleaning

  • Air the room and, if you can, lift the rug off the floor so that the underside dries.
  • Do not pour strong cleaning products on the rug: they can change the colour.
  • Do not wash a wool rug in hot water: hot water together with rubbing makes wool felt, and the rug can shrink.
  • Say in the request when the smell appeared and what happened at that time.

What usually fades and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • everyday use and sweat smell in the pile;
  • the smell of a fresh spilled drink or food;
  • a smell whose source has been dealt with.

May stay

  • tobacco smell, if smoking continues in the room;
  • smell from the backing, the underlay or the floor;
  • mould smell in a porous backing;
  • the smell of an old latex backing.

Frequently asked questions

Will the carpet no longer smell after cleaning?

We cannot promise that. A smell can be reduced, but if the source is in the backing, the floor or the room, it may come back. From the photos and the description, a specialist tells you before the job what to expect.

How do I tell whether the carpet or the room smells?

Smell the carpet from both sides and the place where it usually lies. If the smell stays even when the carpet has been taken out, the source is in the room, and then the room needs treatment.

A new carpet smells of chemicals. Does it need cleaning?

Fibreworks writes that a new carpet with a latex backing may at first smell of ammonia. That is the material, not dirt. Air the room and write to us if the smell does not go.

Can you clean a carpet after a fire?

The carpet can be cleaned, but the smoke smell can come back from the walls and furniture. First the room must be dealt with, then the carpet. Send photos and describe what happened.
