What is different in a business compared with a home

In a business, many people cross one carpet every day, and the dirt builds up in the traffic lanes: by the entrance, in corridors, by lifts, counters and desks. The areas are larger, there are more rooms, and they can be cleared only at certain times. So in the request the most important things are the area in m², the list of rooms and when the rooms are free.

How long a carpet takes to dry depends on the fibre, the pile thickness, the humidity of the room air and ventilation. Plan the cleaning so that the rooms do not need to be in full use for a while afterwards. If the rooms are on several floors or in several buildings, write which ones can be cleared at the same time.

Where it is cleaned: on site or in a workshop

A fitted carpet cannot be lifted, so it is cleaned where it is laid. For individual rugs the fibre decides the place: delicate fibres need controlled wet cleaning and full drying, which an office or a hall cannot provide.

Commercial carpet cleaning and the maker’s instructions

Carpet makers, such as Shaw, advise having a fitted carpet professionally cleaned periodically, usually every 12–18 months depending on foot traffic. The maker’s terms may require proof that this was done, so keep the documents for the flooring, if you have them.

The same maker warns that cleaning that is too wet, without moisture extraction, can cause wrinkles and, in extreme cases, delamination of the backing. In the request the name or the age of the flooring helps, if you know them. More on how often to clean: how often to clean a carpet.

Carpet cleaning in a shop, a gym and other public spaces

In a shop, a bank, a car showroom or a gym the work is essentially the same: a fitted carpet is cleaned on site, individual rugs by fibre. What differs is when the rooms are empty and how much street dirt reaches the carpet. In winter, sand and road salt from shoes build up at the entrance.

For public spaces, also say in the request whether the carpet is in one room or several, which floor they are on, whether there is a lift and where a car can stop.

Before cleaning, remove chairs, small tables and plant pots from the carpet. Mention heavy cabinets and desks on the carpet in the request. Curtains in a business have their own page: curtain cleaning for hotels, restaurants and offices.

Risks to know about before the job

Quick action helps with stains: blot the liquid without rubbing, because rubbing pushes the stain deeper. Chlorine bleach and strong cleaning products can change the colour of the carpet, and solvents can damage the backing. So before the job it is better not to use random products, and to write what has already been used on the stain.

For a wool or hand-made rug, colourfastness must be tested on a hidden spot before wetting it, because the dyes can bleed. In tufted rugs the yarn is fixed with latex glue, and strong wetting and heat can weaken it.

Usually comes out

  • everyday dirt and dust in the traffic lanes;
  • fresh drink and food stains, if they have not been rubbed;
  • greyness by entrances and lifts, if the pile is not worn.

May stay

  • worn or flattened pile in the traffic lanes and under chairs;
  • paint, ink, hair dye and bleach marks;
  • old grease stains;
  • smell, if urine or liquid has reached the backing.

A fresh stain usually comes out better than an old one. Before the job, a specialist tells you what to expect.
