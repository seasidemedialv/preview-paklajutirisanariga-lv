How tiles differ from fitted carpet laid from a roll

Carpet tiles are separate squares that together form the covering. Dirt and wear build up on them just as on fitted carpet laid from a roll: by the doors, in the corridor, by the kitchen and under the chairs. The difference is that one worn tile shows up as a separate patch. If the office is leased and the covering belongs to the owner of the building, ask whether the owner has flooring documents or care instructions.

After the covering was laid, spare tiles may have been left in the office. If there are some, mention it in the request: it is useful to know if a tile has to be replaced later. Replacing a worn or damaged tile is not a cleaning job, and we do not do repairs.

Why a tile covering must not be over-wetted

Makers such as Shaw warn that cleaning a soft floor covering that is too wet, without removing the moisture, can cause wrinkles and, in extreme cases, delamination of the backing. This is why tiles, like fitted carpet, must not be washed with a lot of water.

How long a carpet takes to dry depends on the fibre, the pile thickness, the room humidity and the ventilation. Plan not to use the room for a while after cleaning, and roll chairs with casters back only when the covering is dry.

Office carpet tile cleaning: paths and stains

On tiles, stains behave as on other carpets. Blot the liquid with a clean cloth without rubbing, because rubbing pushes the stain deeper. Chlorine bleach and strong cleaning products can change the colour, and solvents can damage the backing.

Light bleaches dyes. Conservators write this about carpets in general, and on tiles it means that a tile by the window can be paler than a tile in the middle of the room even after cleaning.

How much carpet tile cleaning costs

Carpet tiles are a fitted soft floor covering, so the guide price is the same as for fitted carpet. For businesses the price depends on the area and the volume; the unit prices are the same “from” prices.

  • 45 m² of tiles: 45 × €4 = from €180.
  • 200 m² of tiles: 200 × €2.50 = from €500, because over 100 m² the price applies to the whole area.
  • Only the entrance area, 8 m²: 8 × €4 = €32, so you pay €45.

The minimum order is €45: if the total is lower, you pay €45. Travel is included in Riga and up to about 15 km outside the Riga boundary; further out it is from €10. Frequently asked questions

Do carpet tiles have to be lifted before cleaning?

No. Carpet tiles are cleaned on site, like fitted carpet. Before the job, take chairs, small tables and plant pots off the covering.

Will a cleaned tile look the same as a spare tile?

Not always. Light bleaches dyes, and cleaning does not restore wear, so the tone may differ even after cleaning.

Can only some of the tiles be cleaned, for example by the entrance?

Yes, the price depends on the area. For a small zone the minimum often applies: if the total is lower than €45, you pay €45.

Is the price for a tile covering different from fitted carpet laid from a roll?

The guide price is the same: up to 100 m² from €4/m², over 100 m² from €2.50/m². The final price is affected by the dirt, the stains, access and distance.
