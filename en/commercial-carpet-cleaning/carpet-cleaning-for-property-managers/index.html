Stairwell carpet cleaning

In a stairwell the carpet wears most on the edges of the steps and on the landings by the flat doors, while at the entrance on the ground floor street dirt collects. Access on stairs is narrow, and access is one of the price factors.

For a stair runner, give its width, the number of steps and the sizes of the landings, and add photos from above and from the side. If the runner is fixed with rods or strips, photograph those too. Stairs in a private house have their own page: stair and hallway carpet cleaning.

Residents and timing

A stairwell may be the only entrance to the building, so it cannot be closed for long. Let the residents know the date and time, and say in the request when the stairwell has the fewest people. How long a carpet takes to dry depends on the fibre, the pile thickness, the room humidity and the ventilation.

If a resident reports a stain or a smell on a particular floor, add a photo and write which floor it is on. A smell can be reduced, but we cannot promise it will go.

Entrance hall and entrance mats

In an entrance hall the carpet takes the most street dirt, and in winter sand and road salt come in on shoes. The building’s own entrance mats can be cleaned, and the price is given from a photo and the size. We do not offer an exchange service for replaceable entrance mats.

Carpet cleaning in an office building

In an office building the property manager may be responsible for the shared areas: the entrance hall, corridors, lift lobbies and stairs. If you need a price for the shared areas and for the tenants' premises separately, split the areas in the request. For tenants' offices: office carpet cleaning and carpet tile cleaning in an office.

Makers such as Shaw advise cleaning fitted carpet professionally at regular intervals, usually every 12–18 months, depending on foot traffic. A busy shared corridor may need it more often than a floor with a single tenant.

What a property manager writes in the request

  • The building addresses and the places to clean in each building.
  • The area in m², or the runner length and width and the number of steps.
  • Photos: the stairs, landings, entrance hall, entrance mats, stains.
  • The number of floors, whether there is a lift and where a car can stop.
  • The time when the rooms are free or have the fewest people.

How much carpet cleaning costs for a property manager

For businesses the price depends on the area and the volume; the unit prices are the same “from” prices. Fitted carpet up to 100 m² costs from €4/m², over 100 m² from €2.50/m², and then this price applies to the whole area.

  • Stairwell runners (fixed to the steps) totalling 30 m²: 30 × €4 = from €120.
  • Shared corridors of an office building, 180 m²: 180 × €2.50 = from €450.
  • A single synthetic entrance hall rug of 8 m² (cleaned on site): 8 × €6 = from €48.

The minimum order is €45: if the total is lower, you pay €45. Travel is included in Riga and up to about 15 km outside the Riga boundary; further out it is from €10. What usually comes out and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • street dirt and sand on steps and landings;
  • greyness in the entrance hall and by the lifts;
  • fresh stains, if they have not been rubbed.

May stay

  • worn step edges;
  • old stains that were cleaned with an unsuitable product;
  • a smell, if it comes from the stairwell and not from the carpet;
  • paint and bleach patches.

Frequently asked questions

Can the stairwells of several buildings be requested at once?

Yes. Write the addresses and the sizes of each stairwell in one request, and add photos. A specialist gives the price for each building.

How do I measure a stairwell carpet for the price?

Write the width of the runner, the number of steps and the length and width of the landings in metres, and add photos. From that a specialist gives the price.

Do property managers have special prices?

The unit prices are the same “from” prices. For businesses the price depends on the area and the volume, and for fitted carpet over 100 m², from €2.50/m² applies to the whole area.

Can entrance hall rugs and stair runners be in one request?

Yes. List the entrance hall rugs and the stair runners in one request, with sizes and photos.
