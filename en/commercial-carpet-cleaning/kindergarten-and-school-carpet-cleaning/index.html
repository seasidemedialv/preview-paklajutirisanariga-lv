What the hygiene regulations say

Kindergartens and schools have their own hygiene regulations, but they do not set how often carpets must be cleaned. They require a cleaning plan and wet cleaning, but do not mention carpets separately. How often to clean carpets is decided by the institution in its own cleaning plan.

For pre-school education institutions: Cabinet Regulation No. 890 of 17.09.2013 “Hygiene Requirements for the Providers of Child Supervision Service and Educational Institutions Implementing a Pre-School Education Programme”. Paragraph 23: the floor surface must not be slippery. Paragraph 52: wet cleaning is done according to the institution’s cleaning and disinfection plan; changing rooms, group and play rooms and toilets are wet-cleaned at least twice a day. Compliance is monitored by the Health Inspectorate (Veselības inspekcija). Checked 10.11.2026.

For schools: Cabinet Regulation No. 610 of 27.12.2002 “Hygiene Requirements for Educational Institutions Implementing the General Basic Education, General Secondary Education, Vocational Basic Education, Industrial Education, or Vocational Secondary Education Programmes”. Paragraph 46: rooms are kept clean, and the institution draws up a cleaning plan. Paragraph 47: rooms are wet-cleaned at least once a day, with the exceptions named in the regulations. Checked 10.11.2026.

Group room and play rug: what is different

In a kindergarten children sit and play on the rug, so it gets dirty differently from an office. Food, juice, pencils and sand from shoes end up on a play rug, and sometimes urine too. Play corner rugs tend to be separate and small, and each group may have its own.

With urine, time and depth matter most. Blot fresh urine with a clean cloth without rubbing. If the urine has reached the backing or the floor, the smell can return even after cleaning, and a heavily soaked rug is sometimes better replaced with a new one. More: carpet urine cleaning.

Dust, mites and allergy

In a kindergarten, allergy is an important question. Cleaning reduces the dust and allergens in a carpet, but it is not a treatment; mites return while the air humidity is high. The Health Inspectorate writes that dust mites multiply at a relative humidity of 50–70% and advises airing rooms every day. More: carpet cleaning for allergy sufferers.

School carpet cleaning

In a school, carpets tend to be in the corners of primary classrooms, in libraries, assembly halls and staff rooms. Where there is fitted carpet, the price depends on the area, and over 100 m² the price from €2.50/m² applies to the whole area. In corridors and by the entrance the carpet wears faster, and in winter sand and road salt come in.

If curtains in classrooms or a hall also need cleaning, put them in the same request. For curtains in educational institutions: school and kindergarten curtain cleaning.

When to clean and what to write in the request

In the request, say when the rooms are free: in the summer holidays when the group is closed, or at another time without children. How long a carpet takes to dry depends on the fibre, the pile thickness, the room humidity and the ventilation. Bring the children back to the group room only when the carpet is dry.

  • A list of rooms with the area in m² or the sizes of individual rugs.
  • Photos of the face and the back of each type of rug.
  • Stains and urine: where and how old.
  • The time when the rooms are free and how many rooms can be emptied at once.
  • The entrance, the floor and where a car can stop.

What usually comes out and what may stay

Usually comes out

  • dust and sand in the pile;
  • fresh juice and food stains, if they have not been rubbed;
  • greyness in the play corner.

May stay

  • urine smell, if the urine has reached the backing;
  • paint and felt-tip marks;
  • worn pile by the entrance;
  • dust mites, if the air in the room is humid.

Frequently asked questions

Do the Cabinet regulations require kindergarten carpets to be cleaned at a set frequency?

No. Regulations No. 890 and No. 610 require a cleaning plan and wet cleaning, but do not set how often carpets are cleaned. The institution decides that in its own cleaning plan.

Will cleaning get rid of dust mites in a kindergarten carpet?

We cannot promise that. Cleaning reduces the dust and allergens in a carpet, but mites return while the air humidity is high.

How do I request cleaning for several groups' rugs at once?

Write a list: the group, the rug size and the fibre if you know it, and add photos. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.

Can kindergarten sofas and small mattresses be cleaned too?

We clean carpets and curtains. For cleaning sofas, mattresses and chairs, see mebelutirisanariga.lv.
