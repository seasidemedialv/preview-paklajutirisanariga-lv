What to write in the request

  • Address: street, house number, town.
  • Size of the rug in metres or m². For a round rug, the diameter.
  • The fibre, if you know it: synthetic, wool, cotton, silk, viscose, jute or hide.
  • In the “Where to clean?” field: “At my home”, “With pick-up and return” or “Not sure, please advise”.
  • Stains: what kind, how long ago and what you have already tried to clean them with.
  • Smell or urine, and whether there are pets in the home.
  • Floor, whether there is a lift and where a car can stop.
  • Heavy furniture on the rug, if there is any.
  • If curtains need cleaning too, write that in the same request.

For businesses: give the area of the rooms in m² and when the rooms are free.

Which photos to add

Two shots help: the whole rug from the top, and the underside, or backing, of the rug. If there is a stain, add a close-up, and if there is a care label, add that too. Photos are better without people or documents visible.

Frequently asked questions

Why is there no phone number?

We take requests only in the form, so that sizes and photos do not get lost and a specialist can answer with a specific price. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.

Can I write the request in Russian or English?

Yes. You can write the request in Latvian, English or Russian.

Can I request rug cleaning for someone else?

Yes. Give the address where the rug is, and the phone number of the person to arrange the time with, if they have agreed to this.

What if I do not know the size of the rug?

Measure the length and width with a tape measure, without the fringe. If that is not possible, add a photo with an object of a known size next to the rug.
