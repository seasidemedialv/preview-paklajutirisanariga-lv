The housing estate between the railway and Dzirciema iela

The largest residential estate in Dzirciems lies in the west of the neighbourhood, between the Bolderāja railway line and Dzirciema iela. Next to it are schools, medical buildings, a few private houses and commercial buildings, so one neighbourhood can have different homes and premises with rugs.

If you live in an apartment building, the rug may be fitted across the whole room or a separate rug by the bed or in the hallway. Fitted carpet cannot be carried out, so it is cleaned on site. Large synthetic rugs can be cleaned on site, but wool, hand-made and silk rugs are better cleaned with pick-up, because they need controlled cleaning and complete drying, which the floor of a living room does not provide.

In the request, write which rugs are fitted and which lie loose, how many rooms need cleaning, and give the approximate size of each. If any rug has a smell or urine, state it separately. That way the right option (at home or with pick-up) can be chosen for each rug straight away.

What to write if the flat is close to Dzirciema iela

Dzirciema iela and Jūrmalas gatve connect the neighbourhood with the centre and the western neighbourhoods. In the request, give the address, the floor, whether the building has a lift and where a car can stop by the entrance. If there are few parking places in the yard of the flat, write that.

Add the size of the rug in m² or the length and width, photos of both sides and a note if there is fringe, a smell or visible stains. The nearest neighbours whose pages may be useful are Iļģuciems, Āgenskalns, Ķīpsala and Zasulauks.

Fitted carpet in a flat: once every 12–18 months

Fitted carpet in a flat is an everyday covering, and it has its own care logic. Makers such as Shaw advise periodic professional cleaning, usually once every 12–18 months depending on use. If fitted carpet is wetted too much without extraction, it can wrinkle and in extreme cases delaminate.

The price is from €4/m² up to 100 m². In a two-room flat with 28 m² of fitted carpet that is 28 × €4 = from €112, and travel in Dzirciems is included in the price. Before cleaning, check that there is no heavy furniture on the carpet, and write in the request which rooms need cleaning: kitchen, hallway or room, because the price depends on the area. More: fitted carpet cleaning.

A rental flat between tenants

In Dzirciems, next to the RSU main building at Dārza iela 5, there is the RSU student hall of residence. If you rent or let out a flat, the rug may need cleaning between tenants, and then it is important to write in good time which stains are visible. Before cleaning, photograph any damage if there is some.

In the request, state whether the rug is fitted, how many m² it is and whether it has a smell. If urine or odour treatment is needed, it is an extra line from €3/m². In more detail: rug cleaning in a rental flat.

Kindergarten and school premises

The neighbourhood has educational and medical facilities. If you manage kindergarten or school premises with a rug or curtains, you need to know what the regulations say. The hygiene requirements for pre-school education institutions state that the floor covering must not be slippery and that wet cleaning follows the institution’s cleaning plan.

The regulations do not require rugs to be cleaned at a set interval, so how often to clean is decided by the institution. For businesses the price depends on the area and the volume; the unit prices are the same “from” prices. In the request, state when the premises are free. In more detail: rug cleaning in a kindergarten and school.

Sheers and day curtains in flat windows

Flat windows may have sheers or thin day curtains. If a window faces the sun, remember that light dries out the fibres and fades the colours. The cause of yellowing may be sun, smoke or kitchen fumes, and not every yellowing can be removed, so we do not promise whitening.

Thin curtains are priced from €2.30/m² of fabric. Two sheer curtains, each 1.5 × 2.6 m, are 7.8 m² × €2.30 = €17.94, and you pay €45, because that is under the minimum. If the same request also includes a rug, the total can exceed the minimum. In more detail: sheer curtain cleaning.

Frequently asked questions

How often should fitted carpet in a flat be cleaned?

Makers such as Shaw advise professional cleaning once every 12–18 months, depending on use. For a flat’s carpet the price is from €4/m².

Can a rug be cleaned before a new tenant?

Yes. Fitted carpet is cleaned on site, small rugs can be cleaned with pick-up. In the request, write which stains you can see and whether there is a smell.

Can fitted carpet be cleaned room by room?

Yes. The price is calculated from the total m² to be cleaned, so in the request write which rooms need cleaning and roughly how many m² each is.

Does travel cost extra in Dzirciems?

No, travel in Dzirciems is included in the price. Pick-up and return costs €10 both ways together.
