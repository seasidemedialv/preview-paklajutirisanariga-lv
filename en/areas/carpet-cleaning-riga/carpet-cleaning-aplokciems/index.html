Detached and low-rise houses: several rugs at one address

Aplokciems is a quieter residential area of northern Riga by Lake Ķīšezers and around Jaunciems. It has detached houses, plots with garden cottages, low-rise houses, and a few newer residential buildings. The northern part of the neighbourhood has a golf club, and the rest has residential buildings.

In a detached house the rugs are not only in the living room. If the house has a hallway, a staircase to the upper floor, bedrooms and a children’s room, a list of all the rugs with their sizes helps in the request. That way you can decide in one go which to clean at home and which to take with pick-up.

If a rug fills a large part of the room, it can be hard to take out through the door or down the stairs. So large synthetic rugs and fitted carpet are cleaned on site, while wool and hand-knotted rugs are cleaned in a workshop with pick-up.

An example of a list: in the living room a 3 × 4 m synthetic rug, in the hallway a 0.8 × 4 m runner, in the bedroom a 2 × 3 m wool rug. You can see that the first two are home jobs, while the third is a pick-up job. The price for each is set by the size and the fibre.

Stairs, hallway and runners in a private house

Everyone in the house walks over the stair and hallway carpet. If the stairs have a fitted carpet, it cannot be taken off like a runner, so it is a home job. In more detail: stair and hallway carpet cleaning.

Makers such as Shaw advise periodic professional cleaning of a fitted carpet with hot water extraction, usually every 12–18 months depending on traffic. Over-wetting without extraction can cause wrinkles and, in the worst case, delamination. To get a price, give the floor and the approximate number of steps.

The price of a fitted carpet up to 100 m² is from €4/m². For example, 40 m² in a house: 40 × €4 = from €160.

Before cleaning at home, take chairs, small tables and flower pots off the carpet. Write about heavy furniture on the carpet in the request.

A large rug in the living room that cannot be carried out

If a large rug fills almost the whole living room, pick-up can be awkward. For short-pile synthetic rugs, the work on site costs from €6/m². In more detail: large rug cleaning.

How long a rug takes to dry after cleaning depends on the fibre, the pile thickness, the backing, the room temperature, the air humidity and the ventilation. For a thick or long-pile rug, which holds water longer, ventilation matters more, and the price starts from €10/m².

If the rug is round or oval, write its diameter: the price is calculated by the rectangle around the rug, so a rug 2 m across is 2 × 2 m, or 4 m².

If there is a cat or a dog in the house

If there is a cat or a dog at home, a urine smell can spread wider than the visible stain; veterinary sources note that the area of the smell can be up to three times larger than the stain. If urine has reached the backing or the underlay, cleaning the rug may not end the smell. In the case of repeated marking, the question is about behaviour or health, so talk to a vet.

Smell treatment is from €3/m² on top of the cleaning. For example, a 2 × 3 m synthetic rug at home with urine treatment: 6 × €6 = €36 and 6 × €3 = €18, from €54 in total. In more detail: rug urine stain cleaning.

A children’s room rug and everyday stains

If there are children in the house, the rug can also be a play floor. If something spills, blot up the excess liquid of a fresh stain first; do not rub: rubbing pushes the stain deeper. Put clean absorbent material on the spot and weight it down until it has soaked up the liquid. For blood and protein, cold water works, not hot, because heat sets the stain.

Chlorine bleach and strong products can change the colour, and an unsuitable product can make the dyes bleed. A fresh stain usually comes out better than an old one, but paint and bleach marks often stay. More: children’s rug cleaning and how to remove a stain from a rug.

What to write in a request from Aplokciems

Access to Aplokciems is mostly along local streets connecting to Jaunciema gatve. In the request, write where a car can stop near the house, which floor the rug is on and whether there are stairs. Add photos of the top and the underside, the size in metres, and mention a smell, if there is one.

If curtains also need cleaning, write them in the same request: from €2.30/m² for sheer and day curtains, and from €3.70/m² of fabric for heavy curtains. Two heavy curtains, each 1.5 × 2.6 m of fabric, come to 7.8 m² × €3.70 = €28.86, so the charge is €45 (the minimum order). In more detail: curtain cleaning.

Frequently asked questions

Can a stair carpet in a detached house be cleaned without taking it off?

A fitted stair carpet is cleaned at home, because it cannot be taken off like a runner. Write the number of floors and whether the stairs are straight or have a turn.

How much does travel to Aplokciems cost?

Travel in Aplokciems is included in the price. If you choose pick-up, it costs €10 (both ways together).

Can cat urine be cleaned out of a rug completely?

The smell can be reduced, but we cannot promise that it will disappear. If urine has reached the backing, the smell may come back.

How do I prepare the rugs if there are several in the house?

Write each rug separately with its size, the fibre if you know it, and photos of both sides. Then a specialist can tell which jobs are at home and which are with pick-up.
