A house can have several rugs, runners and curtains, and a summer house a rug that spends part of the year in an unheated room. A synthetic rug is cleaned on site, wool and delicate rugs with pick-up and return.

From a summer house district to year-round homes

The neighbourhood portal writes that because of the closeness to Riga, the former Mangaļi parish in effect became a summer house district. Today the buildings are detached houses, summer houses and low-rise houses. The neighbourhood also has recreation, nature tourism, fishing and port-adjacent services.

This creates two different rug jobs. If the house is lived in all year round, the rugs carry everyday wear: a large rug in the living room, a runner in the hallway, a fitted carpet on the stairs. If it is a summer house, the rug can lie unused for most of the year, and then how it is stored matters. Each case suits a different approach and different pages.

For a summer house and a private house: carpet cleaning in a private house. For small and delicate rugs: rug cleaning with pick-up. For a wool rug, colourfastness should be tested on a hidden spot before wetting, because the dyes can bleed.

If you do not know what the rug is made of, choose “Not sure, please advise” in the form and add photos of both sides. For silk, hand-made and antique rugs, the price is given from a photo. We do not do repairs or fringe restoration, so if the rug has tears, write that in the request.

Narrower access roads through Vecāķi and Vecdaugava

The network of access roads to Mangaļsala is narrower and runs via Vecāķi and Vecdaugava. The area is one of sea wind, dunes, sand, coastal forest and seasonal visitors. Travel to Mangaļsala is included in the price, but in the request write how to reach the house and where a car can be left.

What else to state:

  • whether the house is lived in all year or is a summer house;
  • the length and width of each rug in metres;
  • whether the rug is fitted or loose;
  • which floor it is on and whether the stairs are narrow;
  • whether the rug has been by the sea or the dunes, if there is sand on it;
  • photos of the top and of the underside.

If the rug is picked up, write where it is available, and if it is cleaned on site, write where a car can be parked.

A summer house rug in winter and autumn

If a summer house stands unheated in winter, it is better to dry the rug before autumn and store it in a dry place. Conservators advise against cellars and attics, and say not to fold the rug but to roll it on a tube along the direction of the pile. High humidity damages wool fibres and can make the dyes bleed.

Before storage a wool rug should be cleaned, because moth larvae feed on wool and choose dark places. Live larvae are a pest-control job. Read: how to store a rug and wool rug cleaning.

The LVĢMC preliminary flood risk assessment of 2024 names stretches of the Daugava (Gulf of Riga) in Riga as areas of significant flood risk. So in a coastal house, a rug that has been in a flood or has lain long in a damp room should first be taken out and dried, and only then rolled and stored. In damp conditions a wool rug can have its dyes bleed and its fibres damaged.

A detached house: stairs, hallway and several rugs

In a detached house there can be a rug in almost every room: in the entrance, the hallway, on the stairs, in the living room. Runners wear differently and can be of different fibres. A 0.8 × 3 m runner is 2.4 m², and 2.4 × €6 = €14.40, so you pay €45 (the minimum order). A small rug on its own does not reach the minimum, so in the request write all the rugs together.

More: stair and hallway carpet cleaning and large rug cleaning. Example: a 3 × 4 m synthetic rug at home is 12 m² × €6 = from €72.

Cleaning a rug with snow: what to know

In winter many people clean rugs with dry snow in the yard. This is a habit, not a cleaning method with a confirmed result. If the snow is wet, it soaks in; if the snow in the street has sand or salt in it, these can get into the rug. A wet wool rug that is rubbed can felt; before it is rolled it must be completely dry.

More: cleaning a rug with snow. If a thorough cleaning is needed, a wool rug is cleaned with pick-up, while for a short-pile synthetic rug the price at home is from €6/m².

Frequently asked questions

Can a rug be picked up from a summer house in Mangaļsala in winter too?

Yes, pick-up can also be requested in winter. Pick-up and return costs €10 (both ways together), and travel to Mangaļsala is included in the price. In the request, write when the rug is available.

Can I clean a rug with snow before having it professionally cleaned?

Many people do it, but wet snow soaks in, and for wool moisture together with rubbing is a risk. If the rug is valuable, it is better to request cleaning with pick-up and add photos.

How much does cleaning a large rug in a detached house cost?

For a short-pile synthetic rug the price is from €6/m², for a long-pile (shaggy) or thick one from €10/m². Example: a 2.5 × 3.5 m rug is 8.75 m² × €6 = from €52.50.

Can summer house curtains be cleaned separately from the rugs?

They can, but it is more convenient to write both in one request. For thin and day curtains and sheers the price is from €2.30/m² of fabric, for heavy ones from €3.70/m². The price of curtain take-down and rehanging depends on the situation, and is set by the length and height of the curtain rail, the number and weight of the curtains and the type of fixing.
