Houses with gardens, flats and commercial premises in one neighbourhood

Atgāzene is a small Pārdaugava neighbourhood between the railway, Torņakalns and Bieriņi. It has detached houses and low-rise residential buildings with gardens, a few apartment buildings and commercial areas near the railway. Along Vienības gatve and in the central and southern part of the neighbourhood there are shopping centres, offices and residential buildings.

So there are three kinds of job here. A house with a garden and several rooms has several rugs, of which the large ones are on-site jobs. In a flat the key things are the size of the rug and whether it can be taken out. In an office or a shop it is usually fitted carpet, priced by the area.

Not sure which model suits? In the form, choose “Not sure, please advise” and send photos. Work at home suits a fitted carpet and large synthetic rugs, while pick-up is for wool, silk, viscose and hand-knotted rugs.

Fitted carpet in a flat or an office

A fitted carpet cannot be taken away, so it is cleaned at home or in the premises. Makers such as Shaw advise periodic professional hot water extraction, usually every 12–18 months depending on traffic; before ordering, read your carpet maker’s instructions. Over-wetting without extraction can cause wrinkles and, in the worst case, delamination.

For a flat with a fitted carpet, write the area in square metres, and for an office also whether it is one large room or several. An example: 40 m² in a flat, 40 × €4 = from €160, travel included. About this job: fitted carpet cleaning in Atgāzene.

Tufted or hand-knotted: why it matters before you send a request

A house with a garden can have different rugs: one hand-knotted, another tufted. In a tufted rug the yarn is punched through the backing fabric and held with latex glue, and a second fabric covers it from below. A hand-knotted rug has no glue layer, and the fringe is the ends of the warp threads.

That is why a photo of the underside of a tufted rug matters. Heavy wetting and heat can weaken the glue, and over time the backing can crumble or smell. Where to clean a tufted rug depends on the fibre and the size; the price is set by the fibre (for example, synthetics at home from €6/m²). In more detail: tufted rug cleaning in Atgāzene.

A rental flat: the rug between tenants

If you let a flat, a rug or a fitted carpet may need cleaning between tenants. In the request, add close-up photos of the stains and mention a smell, if there is one. If the flat has to be ready by a certain date, write it in the request. A fresh stain usually comes out better than an old one; paint and bleach marks often stay.

About such jobs, read rug cleaning in a rental flat.

Sheer and day curtains at sunny windows

If a house or flat has sheer or day curtains that hang in the sun all day, the light dries the fibre and fades the colours. Yellowing can be caused by sun, smoke or kitchen steam, and not every yellowing can be removed. Nobody can promise that the fabric will become white again.

For sheer and day curtains the price is from €2.30/m² of fabric. You measure the curtain fabric itself (width times length), not the window. In more detail: sheer curtain cleaning in Atgāzene.

Access and what to write in the form

Vienības gatve, the railway stop and the nearby main roads connect the neighbourhood with the centre and the direction of Mārupe. In the request, write where a car can stop near the house or the office, which floor the space is on and whether there is a lift. Add the size in metres (length times width, without the fringe), photos of both sides and a note about a smell or pets.

Rugs and curtains can be requested at once. If you know the fibre of the rug, state it; if you do not, a photo of the underside helps to identify it, because many rugs have no care label.
