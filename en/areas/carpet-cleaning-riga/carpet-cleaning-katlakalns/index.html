Low-rise development between Bukaišu iela and the Daugava

Katlakalns is a southern Riga neighbourhood by the Daugava and the Ķekava boundary. The area between Bukaišu iela and the Daugava is taken up by low-rise private houses with allotment gardens. The development includes detached houses, low-rise residential houses, gardens and separate commercial sites, and the neighbourhood also has suburban services and some small-scale commercial activity.

If a private house has rugs in several rooms, they can be of different kinds: in the living room, the bedrooms, the hallway and on the stairs. If your house has larger rooms, the rug may be large, and it can be cleaned on site if it is synthetic with a short pile. Wool, cotton and hand-made rugs are cleaned with pick-up.

Because the rugs differ from house to house, in the request write the size of each rug and, if you know it, the fibre. Add photos of the top and the underside, and then a specialist tells you which option to choose.

Stairs and hallway in a private house

Snow in the yard and a winter rug

Some people clean rugs with dry snow in winter, and with a yard it is easy to do. However, for a wool rug moisture can make the dye bleed, and wet wool that is rubbed can felt. The rug must be dried before rolling up, otherwise moisture stays inside the roll.

Street snow can carry sand and salt, and wet snow soaks in deeper than dry. If the rug is valuable or old, send it for cleaning with pick-up. In more detail: cleaning a rug with snow.

A rug stored over summer

If the house has a rug that is to be stored for the warmer season, storage should start from a clean, dry rug. The Canadian Conservation Institute advises not to fold a rug but to roll it in the direction of the pile onto a wide tube, cover it with unbleached cotton cloth and keep it above floor level, not in a cellar or attic. Moth larvae feed on wool and avoid light, so it is better to clean a wool rug before storage.

If the rug already has live moth larvae, that is a pest control job. More: how to store a rug.

A large rug in a large room

In Katlakalns houses the rooms can be larger than in apartment buildings, and the rug covers a large part of the floor. If the rug is synthetic with a short pile, it can be cleaned at home, and it does not need to be carried out. Before cleaning, remove chairs, small tables and plant pots; write about heavy furniture in the request.

For example, a 2.5 × 3 m rug is 7.5 m², and 7.5 m² × €6 = from €45, exactly the minimum. If the rug is a thick long-pile (shaggy) rug, the price is from €10/m², and 7.5 m² × €10 = from €75. In more detail: large rug cleaning.

A cat or dog in a house with a garden

If a cat or a dog lives in the house, urine and hair can get into the rug. Veterinary sources write that the area of the smell can be up to three times larger than the visible patch. If the urine has reached the backing, the smell may come back, and we cannot promise that it will disappear.

Urine or odour treatment is an extra item, from €3/m². For a 3 × 4 m synthetic rug at home that is 12 m² × €6 = €72 for cleaning and 12 m² × €3 = €36 for treatment, from €108 in total. In more detail: rug cleaning for urine and rug cleaning for pet hair.

Frequently asked questions

Can the stair carpet of a private house be cleaned on site?

Yes, fitted stair and hallway carpet is cleaned at home; the price is from €4/m². Travel in Katlakalns is included in the price.

Can a wool rug be stored in an unheated house?

Conservators advise rolling the rug up and keeping it in a dry place, not in a cellar or attic. It is better to clean a wool rug before storage.

May a rug be cleaned with snow in the yard?

Some people do it in winter, but for wool it is a risk: moisture, dye bleeding and felting. A valuable rug is better sent for cleaning with pick-up.

How is pick-up of a wool rug charged?

A wool rug costs from €10/m², and pick-up and return is charged at €10 for both ways together.
