Large plots and low-rise houses

Berģi is a green eastern suburban neighbourhood by Brīvības gatve and around Lake Jugla. Development in Jugla village (Juglas parks) began in the 1920s, when the area was divided into plots of 1,500–2,000 m². Today Berģi is dominated by extensive low-rise housing, with only a few multi-storey blocks.

A house on a large plot has a yard where a rug can be aired, but airing does not replace cleaning. Next to older houses there are also newer private-house projects.

In such housing there can be three kinds of work: large rugs in living rooms, children’s room rugs and stair carpets between floors. Synthetic rugs and a fitted stair carpet are cleaned at home, while wool and hand-knotted rugs go with pick-up.

A children’s room rug: chewing gum, wax and other stains

If chewing gum has stuck to a rug in a children’s room, you can harden it with ice in a plastic bag and scrape off the remainder. Candle wax is first scraped off with a blunt knife. The method with a hot iron and paper exists, but it can push the wax deeper, and heat is a risk for wool and for synthetics that can melt.

If coffee or tea has been spilled on a rug, a fresh stain is blotted, not rubbed. A fresh stain usually comes out better than an old one, but paint and bleach marks often stay. More: children’s rug cleaning in Berģi and how to remove a stain from a rug.

Cleaning a rug with snow in the yard: what it does and where the risk is

In winter many people clean rugs with dry snow. In the yard of a detached house this is easier to do than in a flat, but there are risks. If the rug gets wet, wool can bleed dye, and rubbing can make wool felt. Before the rug is rolled up, it must dry completely.

Street snow can carry sand and salt, and wet snow soaks in. Snow does not replace cleaning the rug. Read about cleaning a rug with snow.

It is better to hand wool and hand-knotted rugs over for cleaning than to wet them at home.

Stair and hallway carpet in a two-storey house

If the house has a stair or hallway carpet, write the number of steps and whether the carpet runs the full length. A fitted carpet cannot be taken to a workshop, so it is cleaned at home. The price up to 100 m² is from €4/m². See stair and hallway carpet cleaning in Berģi.

Makers such as Shaw advise periodic professional cleaning of a fitted carpet, usually every 12–18 months depending on traffic. Stairs and a hallway see more traffic than a bedroom, so they may need cleaning more often. Over-wetting without extraction can cause wrinkles. An example: 40 m² of fitted carpet in a house is 40 × €4 = from €160.

A large rug and how it is measured

In a house with a spacious living room a rug can cover almost the whole floor. Measure the length and width in metres without the fringe and multiply: 3 × 4 m is 12 m², and for short-pile synthetics 12 × €6 = from €72. For a round rug the price is counted by the rectangle around it, so a rug 2 m across is 4 m².

After wet cleaning, the drying time depends on the fibre, the pile density, the backing, the room temperature, the air humidity and the ventilation. In a room with windows that can be opened, ventilation is easier. See large rug cleaning in Berģi.

Curtains in a low-rise house

A low-rise house has many curtains: day curtains and sheers in the living room, heavy or blackout curtains in the bedrooms. For sheer and day curtains the price is from €2.30/m², and for heavy and night curtains from €3.70/m² of fabric. The area of the fabric is measured for the curtain itself, not for the window.

For blackout curtains, makers warn that heat and aggressive washing can damage the coating. For sheers that hang in the sun, light fades the colour, and there may be yellowing that cannot always be removed. More: curtain dry cleaning.

Access and what to write in the form

Brīvības gatve and the A2 (Vidzemes šoseja) connect the neighbourhood with the centre. In the request, write how to reach the house and where a car can stop.

Add a list of the rugs, the size in metres without the fringe, photos of both sides, and write whether there is a smell or pets. For curtains write the size of the fabric, not the size of the window.

Frequently asked questions

Can a rug be cleaned with snow in the yard of the house?

Many people do this in winter, but there are risks: a soaked wool rug can bleed dye, and wet snow soaks in. After that the rug must dry completely.

How do I remove chewing gum from a children’s room rug?

Harden it with ice in a plastic bag and scrape off the remainder. If the rug is wool or delicate, add a photo and write it in the request.

Is travel to Berģi charged extra?

Travel in Berģi is included in the price. Pick-up and return costs €10 (both ways together).

How do I measure a rug for a request in Berģi?

Measure the length and width in metres without the fringe. For a round rug write the diameter, because the price is counted by the rectangle around it.
