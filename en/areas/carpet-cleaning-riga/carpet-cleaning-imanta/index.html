A large synthetic rug or fitted carpet can be cleaned at home; viscose, wool and hand-made rugs are cleaned with pick-up. Send photos of the top and the underside of the rug, and a specialist gives the price.

An estate of about 153 ha and the detached houses next to it

Imanta is one of the largest complex neighbourhoods in Riga, and multi-storey apartment development between Kurzemes prospekts and Anniņmuižas bulvāris takes up about 153 ha. Next to the estate are detached-house zones, shopping centres and new projects. So one neighbourhood has large apartment buildings, private houses and new builds.

If your flat is in the estate, the rug may take up a large part of a small room. Such a rug can be cleaned at home if it is synthetic with a short pile. If the rug is wool, cotton or hand-made, it is better to have it cleaned with pick-up, because such a fibre needs controlled wet cleaning and complete drying.

In detached-house zones, besides the bedroom and living room, there may be rugs on the stairs and in the hallway too; they are cleaned on site. Offices and new flats may have fitted carpet; see the prices section.

Kurzemes prospekts, the railway and courtyards

Access to Imanta is determined by Kurzemes prospekts and Jūrmalas gatve, and the railway and Kārļa Ulmaņa gatve are nearby. In the request give the address, the building’s entrance number, the floor and whether the building has a lift.

Also note where a car can stop by the building, and whether the rug has to be carried out through a narrow stairwell or through a gate. If the building’s yard is closed, mention that too. Add the size of the rug and photos of both sides. If you have several rugs in the house, write the size of each and say which of them are wool or hand-made. Mention heavy furniture on the rug as well. Nearby neighbourhoods with their own pages: Zolitūde, Zasulauks, Kleisti and Beberbeķi.

A large rug that has to be cleaned in place

In Imanta flats a large rug may lie in a room it is hard to carry it out of: the stairwell is narrow or the lift small. In that case cleaning at home is simpler, and there is no pick-up charge. Before cleaning, remove chairs, small tables and plant pots from the rug; write about heavy furniture in the request.

For example, a 2.4 × 3.4 m synthetic rug is 8.16 m², and 8.16 m² × €6 = from €48.96. If the rug is long-pile (shaggy) or thick, the price is from €10/m², and whether it is cleaned at home or with pick-up depends on the rug’s size and fibre. More: large rug cleaning.

Stairs and hallway in detached-house zones

Imanta has detached-house zones where the stairs to the first floor or the hallway may be covered with fitted carpet. Such a covering cannot be taken away, so it is cleaned on site. Up to 100 m² the price is from €4/m²; for stairs and hallway together at 12 m² that is 12 × €4 = from €48.

In the request, write the number of steps and the length of the hallway. If many things have been left on the stairs, remove them before cleaning. More: stair and hallway carpet cleaning.

Sheers and day curtains in a wide window

If the window faces the sun, sheers and thin day curtains are exposed to light. Light dries out the fibres and fades the colours, and yellowing can also come from smoke or kitchen steam. Not every yellowing can be removed, so we do not promise whitening.

For sheer curtains the price is from €2.30/m² of fabric. Four curtains, each 2 × 2.6 m of fabric, are 20.8 m² × €2.30 = from €47.84, and that already exceeds the minimum. In more detail: sheer curtain cleaning.

A viscose rug in a flat in a new project

Imanta also has new projects. If a new flat has a rug sold as “bamboo silk”, it is usually viscose. Wet viscose becomes much weaker and can change shape, and a flattened pile may stay. That is why viscose rugs are cleaned with pick-up.

For viscose, silk, hand-made and Persian rugs the price is from €15/m², the final price from a photo. For a 1.6 × 2.3 m rug that is 3.68 m² × €15 = from €55.20, plus pick-up and return €10. In more detail: viscose rug cleaning.

Prices in Imanta

Fitted carpet up to 100 m² costs from €4/m²: for a 40 m² flat 40 × €4 = from €160. A short-pile synthetic rug at home from €6/m², a wool rug with pick-up from €10/m²: a 2 × 3 m rug is 6 × €10 = €60, plus pick-up and return €10, from €70 in total.

The minimum order is €45: if the total is lower, you pay €45. Travel in Imanta is included in the price. Pick-up and return is charged at €10 for both ways together. If there is old urine in a wool rug, the smell can be reduced, but we cannot promise that it will disappear; urine or odour treatment is extra, from €3/m². If a rug has a bleach or hair-dye mark, it often stays.

Frequently asked questions

Can a rug in an Imanta estate flat be cleaned in place?

Yes, if the rug is synthetic with a short pile or fitted. Travel in Imanta is included in the price. Wool and hand-made rugs are cleaned with pick-up.

How is the price calculated with several rugs?

Each rug is priced by its m² and fibre. If the total is lower than €45, you pay €45.

Is fitted carpet in an office or a flat priced differently?

For fitted carpet up to 100 m² the price is from €4/m², over 100 m² from €2.50/m² for the whole area: 120 m² × €2.50 = from €300. For businesses the price depends on the area and the volume.

How do I find out whether a rug is viscose?

Add photos of the top and the underside, and of the label if there is one. From that a specialist tells you whether it is viscose and gives the price.
