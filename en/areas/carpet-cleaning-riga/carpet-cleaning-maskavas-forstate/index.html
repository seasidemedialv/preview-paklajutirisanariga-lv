Pre-war buildings: what rugs and why with pick-up

The buildings are dense: wooden and brick pre-war buildings, courtyard housing and commercial areas by the market and the railway. The mostly residential blocks are in the south-eastern part of the neighbourhood around Lubānas, Salaspils and Slāvu streets.

If your flat is in an older building, the rooms may have high ceilings, and the floor may be covered with a wool or hand-made rug that you have had for years. Such a rug needs controlled wet cleaning and full drying, which a living room does not provide. Hot water together with rubbing can make wool felt, and the rug can shrink; usually this cannot be reversed.

So the suitable model for such a rug is rug cleaning with pick-up: the rug is cleaned in the workshop and brought back. The price is from €10/m² for wool, pick-up and return €10 both ways together.

Courtyards, the market and paid parking: what to write in the request

Maskavas iela, and the closeness of the Central Station and the market, give good but busy access. Dense historic buildings, the railway and traffic create different conditions from less busy districts. The northern part of Maskavas forštate by the Central Market is in the Riga paid parking zone (B, C), so in the request write where a car can stop by the house and whether the entrance is through a courtyard. Travel to Maskavas forštate is included in the price.

State in the request:

  • which floor the flat is on, whether there is a lift and how wide the stairs are;
  • whether the entrance is from the street or through a courtyard and gates;
  • the length and width of the rug in metres, not counting the fringe;
  • the material, if known, or the label from the underside of the rug;
  • stains, smell and old furniture marks on the rug;
  • photos of the top and of the underside.

Heavy furniture that cannot be taken off the rug must be mentioned in the request.

A hand-made or Persian rug in an old flat

With hand-made, antique and Persian rugs the natural dyes can bleed. So before any wetting, colourfastness should be tested in an inconspicuous spot. If there is no care label, a photo of the underside matters more than a description.

For silk, hand-made and antique rugs the price is set from a photo, from €15/m². Example: a 2 × 3 m silk rug is from €90 plus pick-up €10, the final price from a photo. We do not do repairs or fringe restoration. See Persian rug cleaning and wool rug cleaning.

Tall windows and heavy curtains

In older buildings the windows can be tall, and the curtains longer and heavier. For some natural fibres the length can change by up to about 3% in damp conditions. If the curtain fabric and the lining are of different fibres, read both labels. With velvet curtains, water and pressure can crush the pile, so do not rub them.

The price of curtains is calculated by the area of the fabric, not of the window: for heavy, night and double curtains from €3.70/m². Example: three heavy curtains, each 1.5 × 2.6 m of fabric, are 11.7 m² × €3.70 = €43.29, so you pay €45. The price of take-down and rehanging depends on the situation, and is set by the length and height of the curtain rail and by whether a ladder is needed. Read curtain dry cleaning and curtain cleaning in Maskavas forštate.

Stairwells and a fitted carpet in the rooms of an old building

If a room or a stairwell has a fitted carpet (carpeting), it cannot be taken to the workshop, so it is cleaned on site. The carpet maker Shaw Floors recommends hot water extraction for a fitted carpet at 12–18-month intervals, and excess moisture without extraction can cause wrinkles and delamination. The price up to 100 m² is from €4/m²: 25 m² is 25 × €4 = from €100.

Frequently asked questions

Can a rug from an old building in Maskavas forštate be cleaned at home?

Wool and hand-made rugs are cleaned with pick-up, because wet cleaning and full drying in a living room are not safe. Large synthetic rugs and fitted carpets are cleaned on site. If you do not know, choose “Not sure, please advise” in the form.

How do I find out whether an old rug is wool or synthetic?

There may be no label, so send photos of the top and the underside, and also say how it feels and how heavy it is. A specialist tells you from the photos what to expect. For wool the price is from €10/m², for synthetics with pick-up from €10/m², at home from €6/m².

Does paid parking affect the job?

The northern part of Maskavas forštate by the Central Market is a paid parking zone (B, C), so in the request write where a car can stop by the house. Travel to Maskavas forštate is included in the price. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.

Can an old wool rug be cleaned if it has moths?

If a rug has live moth larvae, that is a pest-control job, not cleaning; pesticides are not an effective solution, and in the long term finding and removing the source helps. Regular vacuuming of the rug and cleaning it before storage reduces the risk. In the request, write what you see in the rug and add photos.
