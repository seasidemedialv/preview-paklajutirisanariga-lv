The neighbourhood has both Soviet-era prefabricated panel buildings and a few surviving pre-war buildings and detached houses. Add photos of both sides of the rug to the request.

Panel buildings in the former manor park

Iļģuciems is a large residential neighbourhood in Pārdaugava with an industrial history. The park of the former manor was built over with panel buildings in the Soviet era, and the southern part of the neighbourhood has the Grīva housing estate. Next to them are a few surviving pre-war buildings, detached houses and industrial areas.

If you live in a panel-building flat, the rooms may be small and the rug covers a large part of the floor. Such a rug is not easy to carry out to the yard, because it can be both heavy and awkward in the stairwell. A short-pile synthetic rug can be cleaned at home, on the floor; wool, cotton and hand-made rugs are better cleaned with pick-up.

In the few pre-war buildings that survive in the neighbourhood, older rugs may have a different backing and dyes. If you do not know the fibre, write that in the request and add photos of both sides: from them it can be determined whether the rug may be cleaned at home.

How to get there and what to request

Iļģuciems is reached via Daugavgrīvas iela, Buļļu iela and the tram lines. So in the request give the exact address, the floor, whether the building has a lift and where a car can stop by the building.

Write the length and width of the rug, whether it is one or several, and whether heavy furniture stands on it. If the rug is to be cleaned with pick-up, also write which floor it is on and whether there is a lift, because that shows how the rug can be taken out. The nearest neighbourhoods with their own pages: Dzirciems, Spilve, Voleri and Bolderāja.

A large rug in a small room

If a room has a large rug that reaches almost to the walls and it cannot be taken out, cleaning at home is more practical. In the request, write whether the room has parquet, laminate or another floor material under the rug, and whether it is possible to open a window. Before cleaning, remove chairs, small tables and flower pots from the rug, and write about heavy furniture, such as a bed or a wardrobe, in the request.

How long a rug takes to dry depends on the fibre, the thickness of the pile, the air humidity of the room and ventilation. So in the request write whether the room can be ventilated. In more detail: large rug cleaning. For example, a 3 × 4 m synthetic rug is 12 m², and 12 m² × €6 = from €72.

An older wool rug from a pre-war house

If your flat is in an old house that has survived among the Soviet-era estates and the rug is inherited, it is worth knowing what conservators say about wool. Heat strips wool of its natural oils and weakens the threads, while light fades the colours, so it is worth turning the rug from time to time so that it wears evenly.

Conservators advise against commercial carpet shampoos on a wool rug, because dirt then attaches to the fibre. For wool rugs the price is from €10/m². More: professional wool rug cleaning.

A child’s room rug

If a child plays in the room, food, drinks and modelling clay can get into the rug. A fresh stain should first be blotted, not rubbed: rubbing pushes the stain deeper. Blood and protein stains are rinsed with cold water, because hot water sets them.

In the request write what has been spilled on the rug and how long ago; a fresh stain usually comes out better than an old one. A short-pile synthetic rug in a child’s room can be cleaned at home from €6/m², but a soft wool rug is better cleaned with pick-up. More: children’s rug cleaning.

Curtains in a panel-building flat window

The windows of a panel-building flat may have sheers and heavier curtains together. For thin curtains and sheers the price is from €2.30/m² of fabric, for heavy ones from €3.70/m². Two heavy curtains, each 1.5 × 2.6 m of fabric, are 7.8 m² × €3.70 = €28.86, so under the minimum, and you pay €45. If the request also includes a rug, the total can exceed the minimum.

Natural fibres can change length in moisture, and the dye can bleed. If the window faces the sun, light fades the colours, so it is worth reading the curtain label before cleaning. In more detail: curtain dry cleaning.

Stairwell and the property manager

In apartment buildings a rug can also be in the stairwell or the entrance area. In that case the request can be submitted by the property manager or a residents' representative, and the price depends on the area and the volume. Write how many floors of stairwell need cleaning, whether the rug is fitted or lies as a runner, and when the stairwell is free. The unit prices are the same “from” prices. More: carpet cleaning for property managers.

Frequently asked questions

Can a large rug in a panel-building flat be cleaned on site?

Yes, a short-pile synthetic rug can be cleaned at home from €6/m². Travel in Iļģuciems is included in the price.

What should I do with a fresh stain while waiting for cleaning?

Blot the liquid with a clean cloth, do not rub and do not use hot water. In the request, write what was spilled and how long ago.

May an old wool rug be cleaned at home?

Wool rugs are cleaned with pick-up, because hot water together with rubbing can felt wool and the rug may shrink. The price is from €10/m², pick-up and return €10.

Do I pay extra for pick-up?

Pick-up and return costs €10 both ways together. Cleaning at home does not need pick-up.
