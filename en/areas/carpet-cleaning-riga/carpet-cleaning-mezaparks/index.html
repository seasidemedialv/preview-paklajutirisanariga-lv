Art Nouveau villas and inter-war detached houses: what rugs there can be

According to Wikipedia, the detached house district in Mežaparks began to be built in 1901 and is one of the first garden city developments in Europe. The buildings mix Art Nouveau villas of the early 20th century and inter-war functionalist detached houses. The neighbourhood lies by Ķīšezers, and it also has sports and cultural venues.

An older villa may have several large rugs on parquet, a hand-made or Persian rug in the living room, a runner on the stairs. If there is parquet under the rug, state that in the request. If you do not know the fibre of a rug, photograph it from both sides: that helps work out whether it is wool, synthetic or something else.

Tram, Meža prospekts and event times: what to write in the request

Access to the Mežaparks area is provided by the tram, Meža prospekts and Kokneses prospekts, and during events the traffic changes significantly. Travel to Mežaparks is included in the price.

Write:

  • the length and width of each rug in metres;
  • the type of floor under the rug, for example parquet;
  • the number of steps up to the rugs and whether the stairs are narrow;
  • whether the way in is complicated, for example through a garden;
  • whether there is an animal or children, and where the stains are;
  • photos of the top, of the underside and of the label.

Heavy furniture on the rug that cannot be removed must be mentioned in the request. Before cleaning in the house, take small tables, chairs and plant pots off the rug. If the rug has to go to the workshop, write which floor it is on, whether there is a lift and whether the stairs are narrow. If the rug is very large or heavy, note that too.

A hand-made and Persian rug in a villa

With hand-made, antique and Persian rugs the natural dyes can bleed, so before any wetting they should be tested in an inconspicuous spot. Conservators do not advise using shop-bought carpet shampoos on important rugs: afterwards dirt can cling to the fibres more. They advise consulting a specialist.

The price for such rugs is given from a photo, from €15/m². Example: a 2 × 3 m Persian rug is from €90, plus pick-up and return €10, the final price from a photo. We do not do repairs or fringe restoration. Read Persian rug cleaning.

A wool rug that has lain in the living room for years

If a living room has a wool rug that has been used for years, it can be not only dirty but also faded by light. Conservators advise turning rugs from time to time so that they wear evenly, because light fades the colours, while heat and high humidity damage the wool fibre. Hot water together with rubbing can make wool felt, and the rug can shrink; this usually cannot be reversed.

So a wool rug goes to the workshop with pick-up, rather than being wetted on the floor. Example: a 2 × 3 m wool rug is 6 m² × €10 = from €60, plus pick-up €10 = from €70. See wool rug cleaning and the advice can you wash a wool rug.

Tall windows and heavy curtains in a villa

In older villas the windows can be large, and the curtains longer and heavier. If a curtain has a lining, the fabric and the lining can be of different fibres, so read both labels. Makers warn that heat can crack the blackout coating. With velvet curtains, water and pressure can crush the pile.

An example of the price for curtains: four heavy curtains, each 1.4 × 2.8 m of fabric, are 4 × 3.92 m² = 15.68 m², and 15.68 × €3.70 = from €58.02. The price of take-down and rehanging depends on the situation and on the length and height of the curtain rail, the number and weight of the curtains and whether a ladder or scaffolding is needed. Frequently asked questions

Is cleaning in the house suitable for a villa rug in Mežaparks?

A large synthetic rug and a fitted carpet are cleaned in the house. Hand-made, wool and delicate rugs are cleaned with pick-up, because they need controlled wet cleaning and full drying. If you are not sure, choose “Not sure, please advise” in the form and add photos.

Can a stair runner be cleaned separately?

It can, but a small runner on its own does not reach the minimum: 0.8 × 3 m is 2.4 m², 2.4 × €6 = €14.40, so you pay €45. So it is best to order runners together with the living room rug.

How much does curtain cleaning cost in a villa with tall windows?

The price is calculated by the area of the fabric, not of the window: for thin and day curtains from €2.30/m², for heavy, night and double curtains from €3.70/m². Curtain take-down and rehanging is priced by the situation. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.

Can I request one rug if it is very old?

You can. In the request, write what you know about it and add photos of both sides. Before the job, a specialist tells you what to expect; for an old, worn or fragile rug we do not promise the result in advance.
