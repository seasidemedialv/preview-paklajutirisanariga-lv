There are detached houses, low-rise buildings, offices, warehouses and commercial areas. Fitted carpets in rooms and large synthetic rugs are cleaned on site, wool and delicate rugs with pick-up. Add photos to the request.

An industrial area with clusters of residential buildings

The apkaimes.lv portal writes that Šampēteris is mostly an industrial area with separate clusters of residential buildings in the northern, eastern and south-eastern parts. In addition there are detached houses and low-rise buildings, offices, warehouses and commercial areas, as well as retail, logistics and hospitality.

For a business and for a house there are two different jobs here. For a business, what matters is the area, the access and the time when the premises are free; for people living in a house, what matters is the material of the rug and whether it has to go to the workshop.

For a business: carpet cleaning for businesses and office carpet cleaning. For a house: rug cleaning with pick-up.

Kārļa Ulmaņa gatve, Lielirbes iela and the airport direction

You can get to Šampēteris along Kārļa Ulmaņa gatve and Lielirbes iela, and also from the airport side. For businesses it is important to write when the premises are free. Travel to Šampēteris is included in the price.

In the request, state:

  • the type of premises: office, shop, hospitality space, house or flat;
  • the area of the fitted carpet in m², or the length and width of the rug in metres;
  • which floor the premises are on and whether there is a lift;
  • where a car can stop and whether access to the site is restricted;
  • the time when the premises are free;
  • photos of the top and of the underside, if the rug is loose.

Heavy furniture and heavy equipment on the rug must be mentioned in the request, and chairs, small tables and plant pots should be taken off before the job.

A fitted carpet in an office and a hospitality space

The neighbourhood has offices and hospitality spaces. If an office or hotel room has a fitted carpet, the total area and the traffic matter. In its care guide, the carpet maker Shaw advises professional hot water extraction about every 12–18 months.

The price up to 100 m² is from €4/m²: 80 m² is 80 × €4 = from €320. Over 100 m², a price from €2.50/m² applies to the whole area: a 130 m² corridor carpet is 130 × €2.50 = from €325. These are “from” prices. Read hotel carpet cleaning and fitted carpet cleaning.

A short-stay rental flat between guests

If you let a flat or a house short-term, guests notice the rug. Cleaning reduces dust and fresh stains, but it often does not remove old marks, paint or bleach marks. If the rug has a smell of smoke or cat urine, cleaning can reduce it, but we cannot promise that it will disappear.

If the premises have to be ready by a certain date, write it in the request, and say how many rugs there are. Read carpet cleaning in a rental flat. If the rug is wool, it is cleaned with pick-up, because hot water with rubbing can make wool felt.

For people living in a house: a wool rug and curtains

In a detached or low-rise house there can be rugs on the stairs, in the hallway and in the living room. Wool, cotton and delicate rugs are cleaned with pick-up, short-pile synthetic ones at home from €6/m². Example: a 2.8 × 3.6 m synthetic rug is 10.08 m² × €6 = from €60.48.

Curtains can be added to the same request: heavy, night and double curtains from €3.70/m² of fabric, thin ones from €2.30/m². See curtain cleaning and carpet cleaning in a private house.

Blackout curtains in hospitality and short-let spaces

If the windows of a hospitality or short-let space are covered by blackout curtains, makers warn: heat (a dryer, hot ironing on the coated side) and hot or aggressive washing can crack or peel the coating. So read the label and write in the request what the curtain fabric is and whether the curtains have a lining.

The price is by the area of the fabric, not of the window: for heavy, night and double curtains from €3.70/m². Example: six night curtains, each 1.4 × 2.6 m of fabric, are 6 × 3.64 m² = 21.84 m², and 21.84 × €3.70 = from €80.81. The price of curtain take-down and rehanging depends on the situation. See blackout curtain cleaning and commercial curtain cleaning.

What a business should write in the first request

For businesses the price depends on the area and the volume, and the unit prices are the same “from” prices. So in the first request the total area is useful, and whether the carpet is fitted or in modular tiles. If the room has carpet tiles, write that and state whether there are spare tiles.

Before the job, a specialist tells you what to expect. More: carpet tile cleaning and carpet cleaning for businesses.

Frequently asked questions

Can a fitted carpet in a warehouse office in Šampēteris be cleaned?

Yes, a fitted carpet is cleaned on site, including in business premises. The price is from €4/m² up to 100 m² and from €2.50/m² for the whole area over 100 m². Travel to Šampēteris is included in the price.

How do I request cleaning if the work in the premises must not be disturbed?

In the request, state when the premises are free and where a car can stop. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.

Is it worth cleaning the rug of a short-stay rental flat more often?

How often to clean depends on the traffic and the number of guests, and we do not set a fixed interval. Makers, for example Shaw, advise cleaning fitted carpets every 12–18 months. More: how often to clean a rug.

Is it worth requesting curtains and rugs for hospitality spaces at the same time?

You can write them in one request: for a fitted carpet the price is from €4/m², for heavy curtains from €3.70/m² of fabric. The price of curtain take-down and rehanging depends on the situation, and is set by the length and height of the curtain rail, the number and weight of the curtains and the type of fixing.
