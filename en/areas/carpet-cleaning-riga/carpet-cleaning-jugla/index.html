Estates, private houses and new terraced houses

Jugla is a large eastern residential neighbourhood by Lake Jugla and Brīvības gatve. The development includes housing estates, detached houses, retail sites and educational institutions. In recent decades new housing projects have been carried out in Jugla, including private houses and terraced houses on Mārkalnes iela and Murjāņu iela.

That means at least three types of home with different rug needs. In an estate flat the rug may be large and take up almost the whole room. A terraced house also has stairs and a hallway, where the carpet may be fitted. A private house may have several rugs of different fibres.

If you are not sure how to clean a rug, choose “Not sure” in the form and add photos: from them a specialist tells you whether the rug should be cleaned at home or with pick-up, and what to expect.

Brīvības gatve, the tram and what to note in the request

Jugla is reached via Brīvības gatve, by tram and from the regional roads that start here. The neighbourhood also has Lake Jugla and forests, with a busy arterial road next to them. In the request give the exact address, the type of house, the floor and whether there is a lift.

Write where a car can stop by the house, and whether the rug has to be carried out through a narrow stairwell. If you have several rugs, state for each the size and whether it is wool, synthetic or fitted. If a rug has fringe, photograph that too. The neighbouring neighbourhoods with their own pages: Berģi, Brekši, Mežciems and Bukulti.

Stairs and hallway in a terraced house

The new terraced houses have stairs to the first floor and a hallway, which may have a fitted carpet or a runner. Such a carpet cannot be taken out and carried away, so it is cleaned on site, and travel in Jugla is included in the price. The price is from €4/m²: for 15 m² of fitted carpet, 15 × €4 = from €60.

In the request, write the number of steps and whether the hallway also has a separate runner. If the terraced house has several floors, state on which floor the fitted carpet is. If renovation is still going on in the new house, state that and the type of stains. More: stair and hallway carpet cleaning and carpet cleaning after renovation.

A child’s room rug

If a child plays in your home, drinks, food or modelling clay can get into the rug, and in a child’s room the rug is where the child sits and lies on the floor. A fresh stain should first be blotted and not rubbed, because rubbing pushes it deeper. For blood and protein stains use cold water, because hot water sets them.

For example, a small 1.6 × 2.3 m wool rug is 3.68 m², and 3.68 × €10 = €36.80, which is under the minimum. The cleaning charge is then €45, and pick-up and return €10 is added, €55 in total. A short-pile synthetic rug costs from €6/m². In more detail: children’s rug cleaning.

Schools and other educational institutions

Jugla has educational institutions, and they have their own premises cleaning requirements. For schools the hygiene regulations provide for a cleaning plan and wet cleaning of rooms at least once a day, while for pre-school institutions the floor surface must not be slippery. The regulations do not mention rugs or a cleaning interval for them, so how often to clean rugs is decided by the institution.

If you want to have rugs or curtains cleaned in a classroom or a group room, write the area and the time when the premises are free. Classrooms with south-facing windows must have sun protection, for example curtains that do not cover the glass when not in use; curtain cleaning is priced from €2.30/m² of fabric. In more detail: rug cleaning in a kindergarten and school and school and kindergarten curtain cleaning.

A new flat and renovation

If renovation of a new home has just been finished, the rug may have dust and glue or paint stains. A fresh stain usually comes out better than an old one, so it is best to send the request soon. Before cleaning, photograph any damage and write which stains are visible.

If the carpet in the new house is fitted in all rooms, the price is calculated from the total area: 40 m² × €4 = from €160. If the total area is over 100 m², the price is from €2.50/m² for the whole area. In the request, write whether there is furniture on the carpet.

Frequently asked questions

Is cleaning a large rug at home possible in an estate flat?

Yes, if the rug is synthetic with a short pile or fitted. The price is from €6/m² for a synthetic rug and from €4/m² for fitted carpet; travel in Jugla is included.

What should I do with a fresh stain before cleaning?

Blot the liquid with a clean material and do not rub. Press a blood stain with a cloth dampened in cold water. In the request, say what was spilled and how long ago.

Can rugs in school premises be cleaned?

Yes, for institutions the price depends on the area and the volume, and the unit prices are the same “from” prices. Write the area and the time when the premises are free.

How are pick-up and return charged?

Pick-up and return is charged at €10 for both ways together in Riga, including Jugla.
