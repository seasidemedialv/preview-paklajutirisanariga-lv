A fitted carpet in a room that is not a flat

Spilve is a large north-western area with an airfield, meadows and warehouses. So a carpet here may be in rooms where the floor is covered with fitted carpet (carpeting): in office and service premises. This kind of fitted carpet cannot be carried outside, so it is cleaned right on the floor.

Makers such as Shaw advise cleaning fitted carpet periodically with hot water extraction, usually every 12–18 months depending on traffic, and state that over-wetting without extraction can cause wrinkles and, in severe cases, delamination of the backing.

In the request, write the area in m², the number of rooms and the time when the rooms are free. Fitted carpet cleaning costs from €4/m² up to 100 m², and above 100 m², the price from €2.50/m² applies to the whole area.

What to write in the request and how to reach the site

In Spilve the main routes are Daugavgrīvas iela and the roads towards Kleisti, but access depends on the particular site. So in the request, say how to get from these streets to the entrance and where a car can stop by the building. If the site is behind a gate or barrier, write that too.

For a warehouse or utility building where the office is on the first floor (one floor up), note the floor and whether there is a lift. Attach photos: for a fitted carpet, an overall view and the stained areas and the traffic lanes where people walk most. If there is a smell, write where it is stronger.

If some of the rugs are loose, for example a wool rug in the manager’s office, they are cleaned with pick-up. In the form, under “Where to clean?”, choose “Not sure” if you are unsure about the fibre, and a specialist will advise from the photos.

A garden-plot cabin and seasonal rugs

In the eastern part of Spilve there are allotment gardens. If a garden plot has a cabin where a rug stays unheated in winter, it is better to clean the rug, dry it and store it in a dry place before the season ends. The Canadian Conservation Institute advises against using cellars and attics, and Washington State University (WSU) writes that moth larvae feed on materials of animal origin, including rugs and curtains, and avoid light.

If a rug has live moth larvae, that is pest control work, not cleaning. WSU does advise cleaning items properly before storage and storing them free of pests, so cleaning before winter is a sensible step. We write more about this in the storage advice.

If the cabin rug is wool or cotton, it is cleaned in the workshop, because these fibres need controlled wet cleaning and full drying. Carpet cleaning in a private house or cabin is also available on site if the rug is synthetic.

A large synthetic rug that cannot be carried out

If a room has a large synthetic rug with a short pile, it can be cleaned on site. Large rug cleaning starts from €6/m². Example: a 4 × 5 m rug is 20 m², 20 × €6 = from €120, and travel to Spilve is included in the price.

How long a rug takes to dry depends on the fibre, the pile thickness, the backing, the room temperature, the air humidity and the ventilation. If the room is large but has few windows, write in the request whether the windows can be opened.

Curtains in large windows and event rooms

If a room has large windows, the curtain fabric can be almost as big as a rug. Curtain cleaning is calculated by fabric area: thin and day curtains, sheers from €2.30/m², heavy, night and double curtains from €3.70/m². Measure the fabric itself, not the window.

For blackout curtains, makers such as Wolf-Gordon warn that heat and aggressive washing can make the coating crack or flake, so read the care label. Light fades colours, and this applies to curtains that the sun shines on every day. If both rugs and curtains need cleaning, write this in one request.

Frequently asked questions

Is a fitted carpet in a warehouse office cleaned on site?

Yes, a fitted carpet is cleaned right on the floor, and travel to Spilve is included in the price. Up to 100 m² the price is from €4/m², and above 100 m² from €2.50/m² for the whole area. For example, 120 m² × €2.50 = from €300. Send the area and photos.

What should I do with a rug from a garden-plot cabin?

Wool and cotton rugs are cleaned with pick-up: pick-up and return costs €10 (both ways together), and cleaning is from €10/m². The rug needs to be dried before storage.

Do you also work in rooms used for events, with several halls?

Yes. For businesses the price depends on the area and the volume; the unit prices are the same “from” prices. In the request, write the number of rooms, the area and the time when the rooms are free.

How is the curtain price calculated for large windows?

By fabric area in m², not by window size. Sheers and day curtains from €2.30/m², heavy curtains from €3.70/m². Two heavy curtains, each 1.5 × 2.6 m of fabric, are 7.8 m² × €3.70 = €28.86, so the minimum applies, €45.
