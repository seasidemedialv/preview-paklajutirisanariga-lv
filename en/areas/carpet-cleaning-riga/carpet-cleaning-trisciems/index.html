Houses with gardens and large rooms

The buildings of Trīsciems are detached houses, gardens and low-rise houses, which means the rooms can be larger than in an apartment building. A larger room may have a larger rug, and such a rug cannot simply be picked up and carried. So large synthetic rugs are cleaned on site, and travel to Trīsciems is included in the price.

If the house also has older inherited rugs, they are usually cleaned in the workshop. The most notable building in the neighbourhood is the Baroque residential house built in the mid-18th century at Jaunciema gatve 171, now a private house. It is one of the oldest houses in the neighbourhood, and in older houses the rugs may be wool or hand-made, which cannot be determined without a photo. So send photos of both sides and state whether the rug has a care label.

Jaunciema gatve and limited public transport

In Trīsciems the main road is Jaunciema gatve and local roads, and the choice of public transport is more limited than in more densely populated parts of Riga. In the request, write how to get to the house and where a car can stop.

Add the rug size in m² (length × width, without the fringe), photos of both sides, a note about stains or smell, and whether the house has pets. If the rugs have to go to the workshop, use rug cleaning with pick-up: pick-up and return costs €10 (both ways together).

A large rug that cannot be lifted: cleaning on site

If the living room has a large synthetic rug with a short pile, it can be cleaned right on the floor. Before that, take chairs, small tables and flowerpots off the rug, and mention heavy furniture on the rug in the request. Large rug cleaning costs from €6/m²: a 3 × 4 m rug is 12 m² × €6 = from €72.

How long a rug takes to dry depends on the fibre, the pile thickness, the backing, the room temperature, the air humidity and the ventilation. A rug dries faster in a room that can be ventilated well, so state whether the room has windows that open or a door to the terrace.

For round and oval rugs the price is calculated by the rectangle around the rug: a round rug 2 m across is 2 × 2 m, or 4 m².

Storing rugs before and after winter

If the house has rugs that are taken up for the season, the way of storing them matters. The Canadian Conservation Institute advises not to fold a rug, because creases leave marks and break threads, but to roll it in the direction of the pile on a wide tube, cover it with unbleached cotton and keep it off the floor. They advise against cellars and attics. Washington State University writes that moth larvae feed on wool and other materials of animal origin and avoid light, and that pesticides do not solve the problem, so the rug must be cleaned and stored so that pests cannot get in.

If a rug has live moth larvae, that is pest control work, not cleaning. If you want to find out when a rug is worth cleaning before storage, read how to store a rug.

A rug in winter and cleaning with snow

In winter many people clean rugs with dry snow, and in the yard of a private house this is a usual method. However, it does not always work: wet snow soaks into the rug, and street snow can contain sand and salt. For a wool rug, moisture can make the dyes bleed, and if it is rubbed wet, the wool can felt. Before rolling, the rug must be completely dry.

More in the article cleaning a rug with snow. If the rug needs more thorough cleaning, it is cleaned in the workshop or on site depending on the fibre.

A hallway and stair runner in a private house

In a house with a garden and woods around it, the hallway runner is where sand, leaves and moisture from shoes end up. If the house has a second floor, the stairs may have fitted carpet or a runner. Stair and hallway carpet cleaning is calculated by area: fitted carpet up to 100 m² costs from €4/m², so 15 m² of stairs and hallway is 15 × €4 = from €60.

Makers such as Shaw advise periodic hot water extraction for fitted carpet, usually every 12–18 months depending on traffic. Before cleaning, it is advisable to vacuum the carpet thoroughly, and in the request write whether the stair runner has a fringe or the runner is laid loose: then it is either work on site or pick-up, depending on the fibre.

Frequently asked questions

Can a rug from a house in Trīsciems be cleaned with pick-up?

Yes, and pick-up and return costs €10 (both ways together). In the request, write the size and how to get to the house, and add photos. If the order has several rugs and curtains, write them in one request.

May a rug be stored in a cellar or an attic?

Conservators advise against using cellars and attics, because a damp place is a risk. A rug needs to be cleaned, dried and stored in a dry place.

How much does cleaning a 3 × 4 m synthetic rug in a house cost?

From €72 (12 m² × €6), and travel to Trīsciems is included. If the rug has to go to the workshop, the price is from €10/m² plus pick-up and return €10.

How can I tell whether a rug is wool?

Check the care label. If there is none, send photos of the top and the underside, and a specialist will say which option suits. If the fibre is not known, in the “Where to clean?” field mark that you are not sure.
