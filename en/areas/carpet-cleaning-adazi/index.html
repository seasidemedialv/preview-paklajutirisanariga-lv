Newer detached houses and apartment-building developments: what rugs are usually here

Ādaži is a town in the north-eastern suburbs of Riga, where newer detached houses sit alongside apartment-building developments, commercial sites and public infrastructure. The town grew around the Gauja manor centre, while the newer part is on the right bank of the Vējupe around the Rembergu manor centre. Homes differ, and so do the rugs: from a large synthetic rug in a new living room to a small hand-made rug brought from another house.

A detached house usually has several rugs, and each may be a different fibre. The living room may have a large synthetic rug that is awkward to carry out, and the study or bedroom a wool rug. The first is cleaned on site, the second with pick-up: hot water and rubbing do not suit wool, because it can felt and shrink, and usually this cannot be reversed.

An apartment-building development may have fitted carpet or one large rug that covers almost the whole room. If you are not sure about the fibre, look at the underside of the rug and describe it in your request: your photos help to choose between at home and pick-up. If in doubt, in the form field “Where to clean?” you can choose “Not sure”, and a specialist will advise from the photos.

The A1 links Ādaži with Riga and Saulkrasti, while towards Carnikava and Kadaga the way runs over the Gauja bridges and local roads. Travel is included up to about 15 km from the Riga boundary; write the exact address. If curtains need cleaning together with the rugs, write both jobs in one request.

  • the area of the rug or fitted carpet in m² (length times width, without the fringe);
  • photos of the top and the underside, and a close-up of the stains;

After renovation: construction dust and a new carpet

If you live in a new building or a house that has just been finished, the rug may have ended up on the floor right after the renovation. If traces of paint, glue or plaster dust are left on the fitted carpet, write about them in your request and attach a close-up. A fresh stain usually comes out better than an old, soaked-in one, but paint and glue may stay. More on this job: carpet cleaning after renovation.

A new tufted rug with a latex backing may smell of ammonia at first; this is not the smell of dirt. In a tufted rug the yarn is punched into the backing fabric and held by latex glue, so heavy wetting and heat can weaken the glue. This is why it is better to judge such a rug from photos than to assume that what suits an old synthetic rug suits it too.

A large rug in the living room that you do not need to carry out

If the living room of a newer detached house is spacious, a large rug can cover the whole seating area. If it is a synthetic rug with a short pile, it is cleaned at home. Before that, take the small tables, chairs and flower pots off it, and write about heavy furniture in your request.

How long a rug takes to dry after cleaning depends on the fibre, the pile thickness, the backing, the room temperature, the air humidity and ventilation. A thick or long-pile (shaggy) rug holds water for longer. For a long-pile (shaggy) rug the price is from €10/m², and whether it is cleaned at home or with pick-up depends on the rug’s size and fibre. More: large rug cleaning and long-pile rug cleaning.

Commercial sites and public buildings: fitted carpet in an office or a shop

Ādaži has commercial sites and public infrastructure. Carpet makers, for example Shaw, advise professional hot-water extraction about once every 12–18 months, depending on the traffic. Soft floor covering that is soaked too much without extraction may wrinkle and, in extreme cases, delaminate.

For fitted carpet over 100 m² the price is from €2.50/m² for the whole area (120 m² × €2.50 = from €300). In your request, write when the rooms are free. More: office carpet cleaning and fitted carpet cleaning.

Frequently asked questions

Can a rug be picked up in Ādaži if I live near Kadaga?

Yes, write the exact address in your request. Pick-up and return in Riga costs €10 (both ways together); in the Riga region up to about 15 km from the Riga boundary it is also €10, and further out another €10 is added.

Does a fitted carpet in a new-build flat need cleaning if it is new?

If it has construction dust or traces of renovation on it, cleaning can help, but paint and glue may stay. Send a photo of the top and a close-up of the stains, and a specialist tells you what to expect.

How much does one room with fitted carpet cost in Ādaži?

Fitted carpet up to 100 m² costs from €4/m². For example, 40 m² is from €160, and travel in Ādaži is included. If the total comes out lower than €45, you pay €45.

Can cat or dog urine on a rug be cleaned at home in Ādaži?

Blot fresh urine with a clean cloth, do not rub. If the urine has reached the backing, the smell may come back even after cleaning. Urine or odour treatment costs extra from €3/m². More: removing urine from a rug.
