Fitted carpet and large synthetic rugs are cleaned on site, wool and delicate rugs with pick-up. Travel in Mārupe is included in the price, pick-up and return costs €10, and photos of both sides of the rug must be attached to your request.

Dense streets of individual houses, terraced houses and new villages

The town is characterised by dense development of individual houses and new villages, and on the Tīraine boundary there is also apartment-building development. Mārupe also has terraced houses, newer apartment-building projects, offices, warehouses and logistics sites. Such a range of homes means that one municipality can have both a flat with one large rug and a two-storey terraced house with several rugs and stairs.

In a house with several rugs they may be of different fibres: synthetic in the living room, wool in the study, tufted in the bedroom. A synthetic rug with a short pile is cleaned at home from €6/m², but wool and cotton rugs with pick-up from €10/m². If you are unsure about the fibre, in the form field “Where to clean?” choose “Not sure”, and a specialist will advise from photos.

An address in one municipality does not always mean the same access conditions. So in your request, write the full address and say whether it is in a dense neighbourhood or on the edge of a village.

Narrow streets in new neighbourhoods: what to state in your request

Kārļa Ulmaņa gatve, the A10, the Riga bypass and the airport access roads provide access to the town, but traffic is heavy at peak hours. The local street network in new residential districts can be narrower and vary in surface and build quality.

So in your request, write whether a car can stop by the house and whether the street is narrow, because addresses within the municipality vary a lot. From photos a specialist can tell whether to clean the rug at home or with pick-up.

A new home after renovation

If a rug was laid in a new terraced house or flat right after renovation, it may carry marks from the work. If paint, glue or dust traces are left on it, write that in your request and attach a close-up. A fresh stain usually comes out better than an old one, but paint and glue may stay.

A new rug with a latex backing may smell of ammonia at first, and that is not dirt. A rug’s latex backing can stick to some new floor finishes, change their colour or crumble, so with a new rug on a new floor it is worth checking what is under the rug. More: carpet cleaning after renovation.

Stairs and hallway in a terraced house

In a two-storey terraced house the whole family walks on the stairs and the hallway runner every day. If the covering is glued to the steps, it is cleaned on site; a separate runner can also be cleaned with pick-up. In your request, write the number of steps and the length and width of the runner in metres, without the fringe.

For fitted carpet the price is from €4/m², and for a synthetic runner at home from €6/m². More: stairs and hallway carpet cleaning.

Curtains in wide windows

If the windows of a new house are large, there can be many curtains. For thin and day curtains and sheers the price is from €2.30/m² of fabric, for heavy, night and double curtains from €3.70/m². In blackout curtains the coating can crack from heat, so they must not be put in a tumble dryer or ironed from the coated side; follow the label.

Example: one heavy curtain 1.5 × 2.6 m of fabric is 3.9 m² × €3.70 = €14.43, so you pay €45 if there is no other work. If there are several curtains and rugs, write everything in one request. More: blackout curtain cleaning.

Offices and logistics sites near the airport

Mārupe has offices, logistics and warehouse sites. In an office, dirt builds up in fitted carpet long before it shows. Carpet makers, for example Shaw, advise professional extraction about once every 12–18 months.

For fitted carpet over 100 m² the price is from €2.50/m² for the whole area, so 120 m² is from €300. In your request, state when the rooms are free. For businesses the price depends on the area and the volume. More: office carpet cleaning.

If a burst pipe has soaked a rug

A new home does not protect against a burst pipe or a washing machine leak. If a rug has been soaked, what matters is what kind of water it was: clean water from a pipe is a different situation from water from a sewer. A rug soaked in sewage water usually has to be thrown away, and so does the underlay if it has been soaked in dirty water.

In porous materials that have become mouldy, mould often cannot be completely cleaned out. We do not offer mould removal or room treatment. For the rug see the page carpet cleaning after a flood.

Prices in Mārupe

The minimum order is €45: if the total is lower, you pay €45, and it applies only to cleaning. Travel in Mārupe is included in the price. Pick-up and return costs €10 (both ways together).

Examples: 40 m² of fitted carpet is 40 × €4 = from €160. A 1.6 × 2.3 m synthetic rug is 3.68 m² × €6 = €22.08, so you pay €45. A 2 × 3 m wool rug with pick-up is 6 m² × €10 = from €60 plus €10, from €70 in total. If a rug is round, the price is calculated by the rectangle around it: a rug 2 m across is 4 m².

Frequently asked questions

Is there a travel charge in Mārupe?

No, travel in Mārupe is included in the price. Pick-up and return costs €10 in both directions together.

Does fitted carpet in a new house need cleaning after renovation?

If the carpet has dust, paint or glue on it, cleaning can help, but paint and glue may stay. Send photos of the top and a close-up of the stains.

Can a company in Mārupe request office carpet cleaning?

Yes. For businesses the price depends on the area and the volume, and the unit prices are the same “from” prices. State the area in m² and the time when the rooms are free.
